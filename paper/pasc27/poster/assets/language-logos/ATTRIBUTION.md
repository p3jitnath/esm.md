# Language logo credits

These original images identify programming languages in the poster comparison figures. The artwork keeps its original paths, colours and proportions. The source records, file sizes and SHA256 values in [provenance.json](provenance.json) identify the exact source files.

The Julia logo is Copyright (c) 2012-2022 Stefan Karpinski and is used unchanged under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) for this noncommercial research poster. Its [official source](https://github.com/JuliaLang/julia-logo-graphics) and [licence notice](julia_logo_license.txt) retain the required credit and terms.

The Fortran logo is Copyright (c) 2020-2022 Fortran-Lang and is used under the [MIT licence](fortran_logo_license.txt). Jacob Williams designed the logo, and Milan Curcic selected its colour. The image comes from the [Fortran-Lang asset repository](https://github.com/fortran-lang/assets).

The Python two-snake logo is a trademark of the Python Software Foundation, with its original design by Tim Parkin. Both poster plots use the [official two-snake SVG](python_snakes.svg) to identify Python. The [rendering copy](python_snakes_render.svg) omits the separate grey shadow ellipse and retains the two original snake paths and gradients. The earlier combined wordmark asset is retained as source artwork. The foundation provides the standalone device and its terms on the [Python logo page](https://www.python.org/community/logos/).

The ASM artwork was supplied by the user for this poster. The unmodified source is [assembly_logo.svg](assembly_logo.svg), and no licence is asserted for the supplied artwork. Its blue polygons, clipping path, colours and proportions remain unchanged. The separate [rendering copy](assembly_logo_outlined.svg) replaces only the live ASM text with vector glyph paths from Nimbus Sans Regular, so each export keeps the same lettering without a font substitution. The provenance record identifies both SVG files, the exact font hash and the conversion tools. No font binary is distributed.
