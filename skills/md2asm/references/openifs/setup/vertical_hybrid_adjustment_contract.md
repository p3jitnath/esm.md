# Vertical hybrid-coordinate adjustment

This raw child implements the selected SUVFE_ADJUST_AB stage in `arpifs/setup/suvert.F90`. Hybrid pressure is A plus B times a reference pressure. A and full-level A have pascal units, B is dimensionless, and eta is a dimensionless vertical coordinate. This stage normalises the integrated B gradient to one and adjusts the integrated A gradient to zero, then constructs consistent full-level A and B. It also computes the source cross-product coefficient C, in pascals.

The application binary interface is `int md2asm_adjust_vertical_hybrid_coordinates(const double half_a[20], const double half_b[20], const double reciprocal_depth[19], const double integral[420], const double *reference_pressure, void *const output[5])`. The six addresses enter in x0 through x5. Output addresses are VDELB[19], VDELA[19], VBF[19], VAF[19] and VC[19], in that order. All five main owners have source bounds one through 19, and every stored word is written. The separate TVAB I/O owners with bounds zero through 19 are not outputs of this stage. All arrays are complete, eight-byte aligned and disjoint; inputs are read-only and caller-owned.

The selected source has 19 model and I/O levels, LVERTFE true, LVFE_ECMWF false and NVFE_INTBC three. Thus LLITER is false and LLA is true. The admitted inputs are the actual owned hybrid A/B coefficients and the positive finite reciprocal eta depths and 20 by 21 RINTBF11 matrix constructed by the qualified owned children. A separately qualified half-scaled A profile covers a physical edge on the same eta/matrix, while zero A is a numerical normalisation edge without a pressure-admissibility claim. Reference pressure is finite and positive, with the selected 101325 Pa value. All consumed values, intermediates and normalisation denominators are finite, and denominators are nonzero. Other vertical schemes and the iterative source branch require separate qualification and are outside this raw interface.

First form each B half-level difference, multiply by reciprocal depth and form the A half-level difference. Integrate the B gradient and divide each B difference by its returned surface value. Reconstruct its gradient and integrate again to obtain full-level B. Construct ZONE from that gradient divided by its surface integral, then retain the source integration of ZONE. Form the A-gradient divided by reference pressure plus ZONE, integrate it, divide its profile by the surface result and subtract ZONE. Retain the next integration in the original order. Reconstruct the A differences by multiplying by reference pressure and dividing by reciprocal depth, reconstruct their gradient, and integrate to obtain full-level A. Finally compute C at each layer as `A_half(j)*B_half(j-1) - A_half(j-1)*B_half(j)`, with two separate products and the subtraction. All seven profile integrations remain present, including two results used only for source diagnostics.

Each integration uses the qualified explicit-buffer ITOP/11 profile child, with positive-zero derivative boundary words and the unchanged accepted strict matrix leaf.
