# Vertical hybrid-coordinate adjustment

This raw child implements the selected SUVFE_ADJUST_AB stage in `arpifs/setup/suvert.F90`. Hybrid pressure is A plus B times a reference pressure. A and full-level A have pascal units, B is dimensionless, and eta is a dimensionless vertical coordinate. This stage normalises the integrated B gradient to one and adjusts the integrated A gradient to zero, then constructs consistent full-level A and B. It also computes the source cross-product coefficient C, in pascals.

First form each B half-level difference, multiply by reciprocal depth and form the A half-level difference. Integrate the B gradient and divide each B difference by its returned surface value. Reconstruct its gradient and integrate again to obtain full-level B. Construct ZONE from that gradient divided by its surface integral, then retain the source integration of ZONE. Form the A-gradient divided by reference pressure plus ZONE, integrate it, divide its profile by the surface result and subtract ZONE. Retain the next integration in the original order. Reconstruct the A differences by multiplying by reference pressure and dividing by reciprocal depth, reconstruct their gradient, and integrate to obtain full-level A. Finally compute C at each layer as `A_half(j)*B_half(j-1) - A_half(j-1)*B_half(j)`, with two separate products and the subtraction.
