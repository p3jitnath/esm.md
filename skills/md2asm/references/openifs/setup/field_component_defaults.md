# Owned Field Component Defaults

A field component describes one atmospheric variable and its places in model arrays. This child applies the fifty defaults declared by YOM_YGFL::TYPE_GFL_COMP to one through 64 consecutive source-layout records. Each record has 368 bytes, of which 240 have declared source defaults. Its blank Fortran strings contain spaces, its grid-point-input flag LGPINGP is true, IGRBCODE is -999, the sixteen MP index words are -99999999, IFID is -1, and PREVIOUS is null. The other declared numerical and logical defaults are positive zero and false. These are configuration values, not an initial atmospheric state.

The entry md2asm_initialize_field_component_defaults receives the first record in x0 and an int32 count in w1. The complete count*368-byte buffer must be mapped, eight-byte aligned and initially zero. This fresh owned-storage policy also gives zero bytes to the 128 bytes without source defaults, including source-unwritten fields and private padding. Those bytes remain unchanged; they are not claimed as physical defaults and any later consumer must first establish its required values. The routine returns w0=0 after applying the declared defaults or w0=2 before mutation for an unsupported count, address, FPCR or nonzero incoming byte. It checks the full range before its first store, so a bad later record does not partly initialize an earlier record.

The typed query records the actual byte offset and width of every declared-default component. It locates PREVIOUS through a genuinely associated typed pointer and a unique pointer-word match, rather than taking LOC of an unassociated pointer. The production body writes its explicit source constants with integer instructions, without importing the compiler's default object or any original module data, type descriptor, vtable, callable or model-state packet. It needs no allocation or external service and has no external symbols or shared-library dependencies.

FPCR must be zero under the frozen arithmetic contract. The leaf preserves FPCR, FPSR, errno, x18, x19 through x29, v8 through v15 and the sixteen-byte aligned stack pointer; it has no stack frame or child call. Registers x0 through x17 are caller scratch registers. The caller owns the records and all later field-map construction. The selected eight-field SUGFL configuration, full MODEL and FIELDS construction, and forecast caller remain separate pending stages.

The separate typed reference reads only genuinely source-initialized members and writes those members to the same fresh owned record. A whole-object Fortran INTENT(OUT) reset also copied undefined source-unwritten bytes; that failed diagnostic and both complete 368-byte records remain archived. No such bytes are masked, copied into production or given a physical default. The accepted comparison covers every record byte under the declared fresh-storage policy.

Three typed source/default cases at counts 1, 8 and 64 pass 26,864 complete record bytes, six immediate ABI/status calls, 1,606 independent string, flag, sentinel and null-pointer relations, and 192 guard bytes. Native ABI masks are zero; the first typed-reference call's x18 change is recorded separately. Four unsupported inputs reject before any mutation, including a nonzero byte in the final record. A standalone assembly _start initializes eight owned records and exits zero through the Linux exit service. Its executable and the production DSO have no shared-library dependencies or undefined symbols. GCC 12.3.0 is the assembler driver. No forecast or original-body count is claimed.
