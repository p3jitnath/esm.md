# Owned radiation forcing

This service follows UPDRGAS with explicit owned inputs. It updates greenhouse-gas mass ratios, in kilograms of gas per kilogram of dry air, and the solar input, in watts per square metre. It reuses the accepted greenhouse and solar interpolation children without changing their arithmetic. An interpolation joins two neighbouring dated values with a straight line. No original module pointer, date global, type table or callable supplies an input or calculation.

`int md2asm_update_owned_radiation_forcing(output, capacity, input_pairs)` receives x0 pointing to writable TERDI176, x1 at least 176, and x2 pointing to seven consecutive address/capacity pairs. Their order is TDYNA456, TERAD2040, TRIP175976, owned constants520, owned climate context2792, runtime16 and optional solar override. Runtime contains four int32 words: NINDAT in YYYYMMDD form, NSTAR2, LNF and reserved. A missing override has address and capacity zero; a present override contains one positive finite FP64 value with capacity at least eight. Required records, the pair table and all ten retained lookup payloads must be mapped, aligned to eight bytes, nonwrapping and disjoint from the output. Input records remain read-only. Return zero means success and two means rejected input before any output store.

The caller supplies the accepted clock's valid calendar date, a positive finite day length and, only on a consumed restart branch, a finite timestep whose rounded source int32 arithmetic fits. Each lookup date table must be strictly increasing and retain the values read by the owned climate constructor. The parent requires counts 450 and 2501, complete GNU rank-one descriptors with element size and span eight, stride and lower bound one, offset minus one, and the corresponding upper bound. It validates descriptor headers and output separation. It does not revalidate the whole monotonic table or the upstream calendar producer. Fixed-year, elapsed-day and solar-override branches retain source-defined unused inputs; they do not impose numerical conditions on an unused timestep or solar lookup.

FPCR, the floating-point control register, must be zero for round-to-nearest with ties to even and gradual underflow. The parent first forms the source decimal year, then performs eight molar-mass divisions in source order. It calls the unchanged gas lookup and performs the eight mass conversions, including the fixed source NO2 volume ratio. It next selects the supplied solar override, owned RI0 or solar interpolation. Dates must lie strictly inside each consumed lookup interval; endpoints are rejected before output mutation because the original clamp route can access outside the table. Each multiply, divide and add rounds separately. Fused arithmetic, reassociation, approximate division and output normalization are excluded.

The nine changed FP64 fields have offsets 16, 24, 32, 40, 56, 64, 72, 80 and 168. The remaining 104 bytes of TERDI stay exact. Constants come from the owned physical-constant producer: RDAY at 40, RI0 at 144, RMD at 160, RMCO2 at 248, RMCH4 at 256, RMN2O at 264, RMNO2 at 288, RMCFC11 at 312, RMCFC12 at 320, RMHCFC22 at 328 and RMCCL4 at 336. TDYNA.LTWOTL is at 20, TERAD.NHINCSOL at 148, and TRIP.NSTADD, TSTEP and NCMIPFIXYR at 16, 200 and 33332. The constructor-owned climate context and its payloads must remain live throughout the call.

The maximum private stack is 336 bytes; both accepted lookup children are frameless. The service preserves x18, required callee-saved registers, low d8–d15, stack alignment, FPCR and errno. It retains the natural arithmetic exception flags in FPSR. Its local 16-byte class wrappers contain only an owned data address and a zero second word, which the accepted children do not read. The raw helper `md2asm_owned_radiation_decimal_year(TDYNA, TRIP, runtime, constants)` returns d0 and requires the same valid consumed scalar inputs; it has no frame or external call. The composed production object graph has no original or external callable dependency.

All 1408 complete output bytes match, every input record and climate owner remains unchanged, and all native ABI masks are zero.
