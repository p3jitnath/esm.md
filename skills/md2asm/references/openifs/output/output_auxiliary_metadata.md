# Selected auxiliary output metadata

The direct entry `md2asm_output_auxiliary_metadata_direct`, also exported as `md2asm_output_auxiliary_metadata_`, replaces the selected wrapper in `fullpos_closure/selected_output_metadata.F90`. Its six original arguments are pointers in x0 through x5 to FPOS, Geometry, Surface, Model, TFPDATA and the int32 lag count. Genuine typed owners and descriptors are required. The selected source has NFPCLI zero and horizontal interpolation disabled; either contrary flag retains the source exit status 109. The lagged branch with lag at least two changes only the TFPDATA pointer to FPOS and skips the resolution check, as the source does.

For the ordinary branch, the model and postprocessor resolution identifiers must agree. If the climatology update flag is true, the month is `MOD(date/100,100)` with signed integer division rounded toward zero. LFPMOIS selects NUDATE; otherwise NINDAT supplies the date. These three original initialized scalar metadata objects remain explicit live inputs. A month from 1 through 12 sets the update flag true; another month, or an initially false flag, sets it false and retains the existing climatology owner. The interpolation-weight flag and its complete object remain unchanged because horizontal interpolation is disabled. The FPOS pointer is stored on every ordinary return.

An active zero-field climatology update first uses the qualified direct header release, then the direct empty physical-request allocator. It creates the eight source allocated-empty request owners and allocates FPBUF with shape `(24,1,86)`, bounds `(1:24,0:0,1:86)`, strides `(1,24,24)` and offset -25. Its complete 16,512-byte FP64 backing remains uninitialised, exactly as the source allocation specifies. The domain count comes from the genuine FPOS USERGEO descriptor and must be 1 through 8. NPROMA 24 and NFPBLOCS 86 are selected-route guards. No field value, interpolation weight, climatology file or forecast payload is produced by this service.

The frame is 144 bytes and stays 16-byte aligned. The entry preserves x18, saved registers, FPCR, FPSR and errno. Its only callable boundaries are the two direct children and libc allocation/error-slot services. The auxiliary DSO requires the qualified direct release DSO and resolves its unique direct header-release name; no original numerical callable or original library dependency is linked. Unsupported geometry or allocation failure exits with raw status 116 outside the supported route. The zero-stack lagged branch uses only caller-saved integer registers. `uint64_t md2asm_output_auxiliary_metadata_count_get(void)` returns completed wrapper calls; the empty-child counter remains distinct.

The source's optional diagnostic-only console writes and hook services are omitted under the declared diagnostic scope. Source allocation choices, dates, logical flags, associated descriptors, complete numerical owners, raw status and the unchanged final-owner and ten-file gates remain required. Unallocated private descriptor words follow the explicit fresh-metadata policy stated above; allocated descriptors are compared in full. No final owner is excluded or normalized.

Six focused comparisons cover the lagged resolution-check bypass, initial and monthly date choices, a false update flag, an invalid month and reinitialisation of allocated owners. Fourteen source/direct ABI probes pass with raw status preserved. Complete read-only input records total 67,399,248 checked bytes, and three returned complete buffers total 49,536 bytes with zero differences. A fixture-only malloc observer gives each source/direct unused owner the same incoming byte pattern before allocation returns; production has no such observer and adds no fill. The original LTRACEFP true control emits its two diagnostic lines during these checks. Four forked calls also retain both source exit-109 guards. Source/interface review and the selected complete forecast check pass.

The [complete forecast check](../../../../../src/openifs/README.md#verification) passes for the selected route. Its declared upstream data and branch limits remain in force.

Its [empty physical-request child](output_empty_physical_request.md) constructs complete associated descriptors without numerical payload values.
