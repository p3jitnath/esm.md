Revision 1 extends the complete [LARCINA contract](larcina.md) to an explicit physical-constant owner and the configured local halo. The [direct entry](../../../../../src/openifs/dynamics/runtime_departure_coordinate_fields.S) is `md2asm_runtime_departure_coordinate_fields`. The [physical input](../../../../../formulations/openifs/dynamics/departure_point_fields.md) is unchanged. The old entry retains its original limits and its complete qualified object. Both thin entry files include one shared assembly body, while the immutable original source is retained locally.

Keep the original 44 address slots in their declared order, and append the borrowed 520-byte physical-constant record at slot 45. The first eight addresses enter in registers, so the new address is at incoming stack byte 288. The qualified physical-constant setup produces RPI, the dimensionless value of pi, at byte zero. Load it from this owner and preserve the source's separately rounded products by two and one half. The complete record is mapped, eight-byte aligned, nonwrapping, read-only and disjoint from all writable owners. The 4096-byte stack frame saves its address at byte 3904, below the saved registers at byte 3936. Every existing ABI, register, alignment, buffer and owner-lifetime obligation remains in force.

The caller supplies its live SL1800 record from the configured local-halo producer. Its demand-active word at byte 1036 must be false. This source-valid condition replaces the old single-process restriction, and the entry calls the qualified [runtime local-halo LASCAW](runtime_local_departure_weights.md). No OpenIFS process-count or RPI global is consumed. All numerical expressions, coefficient choices and remaining direct children are unchanged. The native library imports standard storage services and its accepted direct mathematical helper, with no original OpenIFS routine or state import.

Read column and level counts, map bounds, strides and selected fields from their live producers. The supported numerical range remains 1 through 1024 columns and 4 through 256 levels, subject to the complete child domains, mapped buffers and valid signed 32-bit source indices. The current actual halo producer remains qualified for 2560 physical latitudes and four processes. Its limits do not make the runtime numerical child a universal grid provider. Null, misaligned or overlapping physical owners and an active demand flag take the existing unsupported-route trap before a public write.

Both routes complete eight calls, with zero differing bytes across 11187560 complete returned-owner and raw-status bytes, plus 130050 independent geometry and polynomial relations each. A separate small linear-wind interpolation case adds 66320 exact output/status bytes, 1020 independent constant-field and coordinate relations per route, and two qualified ABI calls. These use the same assembly objects and do not rebuild the main Fortran program. All owners are released, with a 120-second process cap and a 1 GiB resident-memory cap. The [trajectory-weight contract](runtime_trajectory_interpolation_weights.md) also records actual source-plane addressing through these parents.
