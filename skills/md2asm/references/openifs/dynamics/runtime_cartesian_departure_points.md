# Runtime Cartesian Departure Points

Revision 1 extends the complete [Cartesian departure-point contract](cartesian_departure_points.md) to caller-owned constants and the live time-step index. A departure point is the earlier position of air that arrives at the current grid point. The [runtime entry](../../../../../src/openifs/dynamics/runtime_cartesian_departure_points.S) and legacy entry include one shared direct assembly body. Both complete objects preserve their qualified versions, and immutable original source is retained locally. The [physical formulation](../../../../../formulations/openifs/dynamics/cartesian_departure_points.md) is unchanged.

Keep the original 24 address slots and append a 16-byte context at slot 25, incoming stack byte 128. Its first address borrows the 520-byte physical-constant record, whose dimensionless RPI value is at byte zero. Its second address borrows the 360-byte clock, whose signed 32-bit NSTEP value is at byte 36. The actual assembly time loop publishes this index. Clock initialization writes -999, while UPDTIM and NSTAR2 do not produce the live index. Preserve the source comparison with one, including negative and zero steps. The [physical setup](../setup/physical_constant_setup.md) and [clock setup](../setup/model_clock_initialization.md) define these owners.

The new context replaces three RPI global reads and one NSTEP global read. Each complete runtime LARCINA call also receives the physical record at its appended stack slot. All physical arithmetic, predictor and midpoint stages, rounding, diagnostic rotations and reporting angles remain unchanged. The 2048-byte frame stores the three new pointers at bytes 1808, 1816 and 1824, below saved registers at 1856. New records are mapped, eight-byte aligned, nonwrapping, read-only and disjoint from every complete caller output. The entry checks these new boundaries before a numerical operation or public write. Preserve all existing ABI, register, input-domain, coefficient, buffer and owner-lifetime rules. The selected WENO alias forwards to its qualified direct assembly entry.

Dimensions remain runtime inputs. Retain 1 through 1024 columns, 4 through 256 full levels, valid active intervals and 2 through 16 iterations, subject to the existing child domains and admitted winds. The new entry imports no original OpenIFS routine or state. Its production library uses qualified direct mathematical helpers and standard storage and mathematical services. New unsupported context choices take the existing abort path before a public write.

Ten source and ten native calls pass, with zero differing bytes across 1979080 complete returned-owner and raw-status bytes and 39466 independent sphere and tangent-basis relations per route. The cases cover P17/L137 and P8/L5, full, final-two and empty work, live steps -999, 0 and 2, saved points, rotation and numerical reporting. Native ABI masks are zero, and natural status and errno agree. Six context refusals preserve 13234440 shared owner bytes. Inputs and guards remain intact and all owners are released. Each process has a 120-second and 1 GiB resident-memory bound. These component wind inputs use NAS4096. The [trajectory-weight contract](runtime_trajectory_interpolation_weights.md) adds checks with actual source-plane widths. Complete weather-state ownership, the configured caller join and the autonomous forecast remain separate checks. No main Fortran program or accepted child bank is rebuilt or replayed.
