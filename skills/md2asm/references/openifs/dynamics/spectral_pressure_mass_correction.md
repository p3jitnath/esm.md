# Spectral Pressure Mass Correction Contract

This contract implements equation SPM-1 from the [formulation](../../../../../formulations/openifs/dynamics/spectral_pressure_mass_correction.md). The source is OpenIFS 48.1.1 `arpifs/adiab/spcmascor.F90`. The caller retains the original model and its supplied dimensionless GMASSINC. No new mass estimate or physical rule is introduced.

The application binary interface, or ABI, defines arguments and preserved registers. `md2asm_runtime_spectral_pressure_mass_correction` receives the original three pointers in x0 through x2. They name the complete 576-byte TLAP wave-map record, the live TDYN dynamics record and a 64-byte GNU pressure-array descriptor. TDYN has 4,720 bytes in the qualified build. This child consumes only its first 4,624 bytes, reading GMASSINC at byte 4,616. Each record is mapped, eight-byte aligned and separate from the others.

TLAP byte 64 contains the complete NASM0 descriptor. Its data are four-byte integers, aligned to four bytes, with lower bound zero, unit stride and one through 8,192 entries. NASM0(0) gives the one-based position of the constant coefficient. Pressure is a separate, dense, eight-byte-aligned FP64 array with lower bound one and one through 134,217,728 values. FP64 means a 64-bit floating-point value. Both complete payload spans are mapped and nonwrapping. Pressure must not overlap a record or the read-only map payload.

Read the one-based coefficient index, require it to lie within pressure, then load that pressure value and GMASSINC. Apply one FP64 subtraction and store its result at the same position. Every other pressure byte and every input-record byte stays unchanged. Array descriptors retain their original representation. The floating-point control register FPCR must be zero, which selects round-to-nearest with ties to even and gradual underflow. Preserve signed zeros and the natural floating-point status FPSR. There is no fused arithmetic, contraction or reassociation.

Return zero on success or two on a checked invalid pointer, shape, span, overlap or index. Refusal happens before any floating-point operation or output write. The child is a leaf routine, with no stack frame, allocation, original OpenIFS call or external service. It preserves x18, x19 through x30, the required vector-register halves and errno, the operating system error number.

The caller applies the complete original scheduling condition. Configuration is A or I, LMASCOR is true, LGPMASCOR is false, the process owns surface pressure, its wave group owns degree zero and the current iteration equals the final iteration. Apply this child to the private pressure copy before the implicit spectral update. The enclosing runtime caller and forecast remain separate acceptance scopes.

One small comparison passes 12 original calls and 18 native calls, including six refusals. The checked array lengths range from one through 4,096. Complete pressure outputs agree, and the compared pressure and unchanged input spans cover 172,912 bytes. Immediate ABI masks, FPCR, FPSR and errno agree. Independent checks cover the rounded subtraction, the corresponding exponential pressure ratio and every unchanged coefficient. Signed zeros, subnormal values, final-element writes, descriptor bounds, invalid indices, overlap refusal and all 90 guarded-region releases pass. The complete spectral caller and operational forecast are not established by these component results. Raw records and build commands remain local under `.runtime/`.
