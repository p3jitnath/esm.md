# Aerosol Optical Mixture Contract

Revision 1.0 uses [P01-P03](../../../../../formulations/openifs/physics/aerosol_optical_mixture.md) and the accepted [extensive forward-scattering adjustment](delta_eddington_extensive.md). The original calculation is radiation_aerosol_optics::add_aerosol_optics. The direct-input body and radiation_aerosol_optics_data::calc_rh_index are complete supporting assembly entries. They are not separate entries in the audited weather queue.

## Numerical Update

Loop over the supplied aerosol layers and active columns. Obtain water mass fraction with the accepted [gas conversion entry](gas_unit_conversion.md), divide it by liquid-water saturation and select the supplied humidity bin. The search uses strict greater-than comparisons. A humidity exactly on a boundary uses the lower bin. A value above the last boundary uses the last bin. A disabled hydrophilic table returns index zero and is never used for a hydrophilic particle. Enabled tables have at least two ordered boundaries, including the same valid search sentinel as the original interface.

For each particle type, form the layer mass with a separately rounded pressure subtraction and division by the frozen gravitational constant. Multiply by particle fraction, then mass extinction. Add the resulting extinction, scattering and direction-weighted terms to the band accumulators in type order, starting from positive zero. Hydrophobic and hydrophilic tables keep their declared indexing. Ignored types have no arithmetic contribution. When longwave scattering is disabled, multiply each extinction by the separately rounded expression one minus its scattering fraction before adding it.

For a direct input, form scattering by multiplying its extinction and scattering fraction, then form the directional moment by multiplying this result by its direction factor. Apply the accepted extensive delta adjustment per band to shortwave aerosols unless scaling is deferred to the gas mixture. Always apply it to longwave aerosols when longwave aerosol scattering is enabled. The adjustment uses its unchanged ordered division, multiplication, subtraction and addition stages.

Initialise the complete shortwave direction output to positive zero. Also initialise both complete longwave scattering outputs when longwave aerosol scattering is enabled. For shortwave, add a layer only when its first aerosol band has positive adjusted extinction, matching the original condition. For each radiation point, map its band, add gas and aerosol extinction, form gas scattering with one multiplication and add aerosol scattering, then divide the directional moment by total scattering and total scattering by extinction. Longwave scattering updates only bands with positive adjusted aerosol extinction and uses zero direction when scattering is zero. The absorption-only longwave route always adds its band depth. The direct-input route obeys separate shortwave and longwave enable flags. The mass-input route preserves the original complete stage even when those flags differ.

## Interface And Storage

The application binary interface (ABI) is GNU Fortran on little-endian AArch64. The parent receives thirteen by-address arguments in source order, with x0-x7 followed by five eight-byte stack argument slots. These are level count, first column, last column, configuration, thermodynamics, gas, aerosol and six contiguous FP64 output arrays in longwave extinction, longwave scattering fraction, longwave direction, shortwave extinction, shortwave scattering fraction and shortwave direction order. Outputs have radiation point as the fastest index, then level, then the active column range. When aerosol longwave scattering is disabled, its two output owners can have zero extent and null pointers, or their complete longwave extent can remain allocated for the cloud solver. Neither numerical branch reads or writes those inactive owners. Compare every retained byte when an owner is present, and do not dereference a zero-extent owner. A direct entry omits thermodynamics and gas and therefore has eleven arguments. The humidity function receives a two-pointer class wrapper and an FP64 humidity pointer and returns an INTEGER4 index in w0.

The frozen configuration occupies 20,040 bytes. Its aerosol table object begins at byte 11,608 and occupies 6,232 bytes. Longwave and shortwave band-map descriptors begin at 10,864 and 10,928. INTEGER4 fields for shortwave points, longwave points, shortwave bands, longwave bands, longwave scattering points and longwave scattering bands begin at 19,996, 20,000, 20,004, 20,008, 20,020 and 20,024. Logical fields for longwave aerosol scattering, deferred shortwave scaling, longwave enable and shortwave enable begin at 612, 628, 6,848 and 6,852. The aerosol type count begins at 6,912.

The aerosol object occupies 800 bytes. Its seven rank-three descriptors begin at multiples of 112 bytes in mixing ratio, shortwave extinction, shortwave fraction, shortwave direction, longwave extinction, longwave fraction and longwave direction order. Its direct-input flag begins at 792. The thermodynamics object contains three rank-two descriptors at 0, 88 and 176 for pressure, temperature and liquid saturation. The gas object and class wrapper use the accepted gas contract. The humidity table descriptor begins at aerosol-table byte 2,128, with bin count at 6,204 and its enable flag at 6,220. The species class and type-map descriptors begin at table bytes 0 and 64. The six hydrophobic rank-two descriptors begin at 128, 216, 304, 392, 480 and 568. The six hydrophilic rank-three descriptors begin at 656, 768, 880, 992, 1,104 and 1,216.

Each allocatable descriptor has an eight-byte data pointer at 0, signed element offset at 8 and dimension records at 40 plus 24 times the dimension index. A dimension record gives signed element stride, lower bound and upper bound. Address each supplied allocatable value from its complete signed offset and strides. Numerical arrays are eight-byte aligned. Output arrays are disjoint and cannot overlap read-only tables, state or scalar arguments. Supplied allocation sizes and band maps must match their configuration. Active columns and aerosol layers lie inside every corresponding input and output allocation.

Support zero levels, empty column ranges, up to 256 levels, 512 active columns, 64 bands in each spectrum and 4,096 radiation points in each spectrum. Enabled spectra have at least one band. Only the temporary water mass matrix and six band accumulators are allocated privately. Preserve x19-x29, stack alignment and the link register. Use caller-saved floating-point registers. The driver supplies no omitted numerical stage. The native closure calls only accepted native gas and delta entries and the standard allocation/free services.

## Arithmetic And Acceptance

Use FP64 round-to-nearest with ties to even, gradual underflow and preserved signed zeros with FPCR zero. Preserve each subtraction, division, multiplication and addition in its original order. No fused operation, reassociation, approximate instruction or reduced precision is allowed. Read the gravitational value from the frozen model constant. Finite, physically valid inputs must produce finite defined scattering fractions, so a positive shortwave mixture has positive total extinction and scattering depth. Undefined model table types and malformed allocations are outside the declared scientific interface and retain local control diagnostics rather than supplying an alternate numerical result.

One small verifier compares all six output owners, unchanged input owners and metadata against the strict original and checks independent extinction, scattering, direction and absorption identities using higher-precision accumulations with an explicit rounding budget. Its critical cases cover particle classes, humidity ties, spectral maps, delta modes, zero aerosol, subset layers and columns, empty work, odd point counts, signed zeros and small positive values. The complete forecast relay compares the same original and native input on each reached parent call. Critical and forecast evidence will be appended after those checks pass.
