# Runtime Ozone Chemistry Tendency

This entry implements the original O3CHEM calculation for the retained seven-coefficient chemistry model. A tendency is the rate at which a field changes. The routine limits the temperature used by the chemistry model, calculates a new ozone value, adds its contribution to the column above the next layer and returns the ozone change per second. It preserves the original physics, including the positive ozone lower limit and its solar calculations.

The application binary interface is `md2asm_calculate_runtime_ozone_tendency(G, MODEL, controls, constants, solar, fields, capacity)` in x0 through x6. G is the complete 20,400-byte Geometry record and MODEL is the complete 4,275,088-byte model record. Controls has four int32 words for the first active column, last active column, source time-stage selector one and coefficient count seven. Constants is the explicit 520-byte physical record. Solar has two borrowed pointers to P FP64 latitude-sine and solar-angle values. Fields has seven pointers in the original order for half-level pressure, full-level pressure, chemistry coefficients, layer pressure difference, temperature, old ozone and output tendency. Pressure is in pascals, temperature is in kelvins and time is in seconds. All records and arrays require eight-byte alignment and complete mapped bounds. Output must be disjoint from every complete borrowed owner.

The live column count P ranges from 1 through 128 and the layer count L ranges from 1 through 512. These limits bound the private stack storage. Ordinary fields need 8PL bytes, half-level pressure needs 8P(L+1) bytes, coefficients need 7 times 8PL bytes and each solar vector needs 8P bytes. Capacity must cover the complete output field. Active columns range from the first through the last, with a supported empty interval. The selected physical flags retain HLO safe chemistry with seven coefficients, one monthly two-dimensional group and no three-dimensional group. These physical choices do not fix the grid dimensions.

The aligned stack frame needs 512+2(8PL)+2(8P) bytes, rounded up to a multiple of 16. Its maximum is 1,051,136 bytes, which the caller must provide as available stack space. Its two complete field buffers hold upper-layer pressure depths and new ozone. Two row vectors hold the accumulated column and solar factors. The routine preserves x18, Arm saved integer registers and the low halves of d8 through d15. It calls only the public tan and acos mathematical functions. Return zero means success. Return two rejects unsupported input before any output change. FPCR must be zero. The original floating-point instruction sequence, rounding points and math-call order remain unchanged, and natural FPSR and errno propagate through the return.

The same object matches 94,440 complete output bytes at 17 columns with 137 layers and at 8 columns with 5 layers. Four new source calls cover full, final-two, shifted and empty work. The original O3CHEM binary remains unchanged, with one shape-aware verification bridge. Independent 90-digit ozone-balance and lower-limit checks cover 7,538 relations, while 5,707 unchanged output words remain exact. The retained controlled floor bank also matches 18,632 of those bytes with the new scratch addresses. Its earlier independent checks cover 411 floor activations and both temperature-clipping ties without another original call. All 21 new ABI and raw-state observations, eight atomic refusals, input-preservation checks and buffer guards pass. These are component results. The coefficient producer, complete physics parent and full forecast remain separate.
