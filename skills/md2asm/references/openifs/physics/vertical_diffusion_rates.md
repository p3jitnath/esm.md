This contract retains VDFMAIN and VDFOUTER from `arpifs/phys_ec/vdfmain.F90` and `arpifs/phys_ec/vdfouter.F90` in OpenIFS 48.1.1. The [formulation](../../../../../formulations/openifs/physics/vertical_diffusion_rates.md) groups unchanged physics. Current primary entries are `md2asm_vertical_diffusion_main_sea_r2_7358525` and `md2asm_vertical_diffusion_outer_sea_r2_7358525`. Both selected routes pass through the retained parent proof and joint forecast gate.

VDFMAIN prepares static energy and total water, performs surface exchange, locates and corrects the boundary layer, calculates mixing coefficients and retains the selected orographic drag. Its Wood predictor-corrector and scaled overimplicit branches retain their original coefficients and ordered child calls. Momentum and heat/water diffusion, state and rate blends, surface diagnostics and closing moisture transfers remain inside the complete assembly route. Pressure thickness divided by gravity supplies layer mass when reconstructing interface heat and water fluxes. Clear the complete turbulence-energy-rate owner where the source requires it, including inactive rows.

VDFOUTER copies seven incoming rates, calls complete VDFMAIN and subtracts those incoming rates from the updated fields. Thus its returned values are diffusion contributions, rather than the total updated rates. Keep the two complete preparation loops and the two closing loops in source order, with one rounding point for each subtraction. Every numerical child uses direct assembly. The sea-R2 bindings retain the corrected salt derivative and all unchanged child science.

The application binary interface, or ABI, defines argument and register duties. The first eight addresses use x0 through x7, the rest and final length use consecutive eight-byte incoming stack slots. CDCONF has length one. Frames have 49,152 and 4,096 bytes. Public numerical arrays are raw column-first buffers. VDFMAIN constructs typed GNU descriptors for surface calls, preserving source bounds, element sizes and strides. Cell fields and interface fields have distinct 19-level and 20-interface extents in the selected forecast. Soil and tile fields retain four layers and nine tiles.

Every used view must fit its complete owner, preserving aliases, readonly records and source-defined inactive outputs. The supported selected calls include full 24-column and partial eight-column intervals; empty intervals are outside the frozen VDFOUTER scope. Preserve x18, x19 through x29, the return address and required low halves of v8 through v15, with 16-byte stack alignment. Require FPCR zero, round-to-nearest with ties to even, gradual underflow, signed zeros and all original FP64 rounding points. Fused operations, contraction, reassociation and approximate arithmetic are forbidden.

Original profiling, readonly constant storage and declared surface-record metadata services remain dependencies. The surrounding Fortran forecast still constructs inputs. The retained independent child, complete-owner, physical-composition, raw-status and native-ABI proofs combine with direct-stage completion and the passing joint final-state and saved-file gate.
