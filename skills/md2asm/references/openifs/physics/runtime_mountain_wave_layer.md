# Runtime Mountain Wave Layer

This component connects the original GWDRAG_LAYER interface to the complete [mountain-wave wind drag calculation](gwdrag.md). Wind over uneven land produces waves, and this calculation forms the coefficients that slow and turn the wind. The shared [argument views](../../../../../src/openifs/physics/shared_mountain_wave_layer_arguments.inc.S) retain the original field order in both the fixed and [runtime callers](../../../../../src/openifs/physics/runtime_mountain_wave_layer.S). The original fixed entry remains unchanged.

The application binary interface, or ABI, defines argument locations and saved registers. The original eight addresses remain in x0 through x7: YDSURF, YDEPHLI, YDEGWD, KDIM, PAUX, STATE, PSURF and AUXL. A ninth address at the incoming stack pointer names a mapped 64-byte context. Its first address names the initialized 520-byte physical record. The address at byte 8 names the four-byte step number, and the address at byte 16 names the 16-byte false-hook policy. The policy contains the address of a false four-byte hook flag at byte 0 and a zero reserved word at byte 8. The caller supplies read-only TEPHLI and TEGWD parameter records of 120 and 96 bytes; this component does not construct the complete enclosing physics setup.

Let $`P`$ be the live column count and $`L`$ be the atmospheric layer count. Read the first and last columns from KDIM bytes 0 and 4, P from byte 12 and L from byte 48; the record has 192 mapped bytes. Admit P from 1 through 128 and L from 6 through 512, with full, cropped or empty column intervals. Retain complete column-first field descriptors, their original bounds and the physical restrictions of the linked mountain-wave children. FP64 means a 64-bit floating-point number. These owners require eight-byte alignment, integer scalars require four-byte alignment and all declared ranges must remain mapped. The 8192-byte stack frame preserves x18, x19 through x29, the lower 64 bits of vector registers v8 through v15 and 16-byte stack alignment. Return zero means success. Return two refuses a new runtime control before any public write. The unchanged nonzero-FPCR route aborts.

The physical address must equal md2asm_owned_initial_physical_constants, and the completed producer flag at byte 512 must be one. The owning assembly entry must use the accepted [physical producer](../setup/physical_constant_setup.md) and [alias declarations](../setup/physical_constant_aliases.md), with each child bound to that same owner. Gravity is at byte 112, while the dry-air gas constant and heat capacity are at bytes 184 and 200. Their units are metres per second squared and joules per kilogram per kelvin. The component checks the actual native alias addresses against these three offsets, and symbolic binding prevents an external source library from replacing them. Production must retain that address identity. An original OpenIFS global does not satisfy this ownership rule.

Complete GWDRAG, GWSETUP and GWPROFIL objects and their mathematical helpers retain their accepted arithmetic. For an empty interval, original GWSETUP still computes $`1/RD`$, $`RG^2/RCPD`$ and $`1/(2RG)`$, where RG is gravity, RD is the dry-air gas constant and RCPD is its heat capacity. The enclosing assembly retains these separately rounded equations because the older child returns before that source prefix. Their natural exception flags are preserved without writing or normalizing the flags. Removing only this empty conditional reproduces the preceding caller source exactly.

One unchanged native object and one unchanged original wrapper binary pass six cases at 17 columns/137 layers and 8 columns/7 layers, each with full, final-two and empty intervals. All 749,232 complete fixture bytes match, with 20,088 independent 110-digit relations across the source and native routes. Inactive outputs, guard bytes and all 520 physical-owner bytes remain unchanged. Twelve nominal calls have matching immediate FPCR, FPSR and errno. FPCR controls floating-point rounding, FPSR records arithmetic flags and errno is the C error indicator. Every direct ABI mask is zero; the original verification source retains its observed x18 mask 2. Six new control refusals preserve 37,968 complete fixture bytes each. Unchanged numerical and boundary evidence is reused, and passed cases are not replayed.

This is one qualified runtime component. Complete CALLPAR forwarding, live enclosing parameter association and the autonomous operational forecast remain unfinished. The surface-time adapter separately needs its owned hook and registry associations. No comparative timing is claimed.
