## Interface And Supported State

The Arm application binary interface, or ABI, defines where arguments are passed and which registers a function preserves. This entry has nine ordinary pointers. The first eight are in x0 through x7. The ninth is at the incoming stack pointer. Their order is GEOMETRY, TSURF, MODEL, KSTEP, PTDT, PTSTEP, LDCLUPD, optional LDUPDECAEC and optional PI05. GEOMETRY contains 17,096 bytes, TSURF 18,224 bytes and MODEL 4,275,088 bytes. Step and logical arguments own four bytes. Each time or optional solar value owns eight bytes. Optional arguments use a null pointer when absent. Scalar inputs and geometry are read-only. The selected route leaves TSURF unchanged.

The selected route has two time levels, complete ECMWF physics, T21 resolution, nineteen atmospheric layers and a one-hour step. It has NAERO zero, NOZOCL one, NGHGRAD twenty-one, NAERMACC one, NHINCSOL four and radiation frequency one. Solar times are centred. Gas, new-aerosol and ordinary solar updates are enabled. Alternative calendar repetition, astronomical year reset, aquaplanet, simplified physics, Moon, nudging, coupled ocean, online chemistry, sulphate history, future solar-angle and ultraviolet updates are disabled. CMIP ozone selection is zero. Unsupported enabled modes must fail before caller storage changes. The original optional presence and scalar bits remain inputs. This revision initially accepts absent PI05, positive finite PTDT and PTSTEP equal to the one-hour stored TSTEP, and KSTEP from zero through twenty-five, including the closing clock update required by the coarse forecast.

A genuine original packet supplies all selected original owners. Its entry-arg0 is GEOMETRY, entry-arg1 is TSURF and entry-arg2 is MODEL. The separate patterned forwarding fixture proves pointer forwarding only. It supplies no scientific values. The measured member layout and source-field mapping are local and immutable for this target.

## Complete Ordered Update

Use the original round-to-nearest-away-from-zero integer conversion for NINT. Ordinary FP64 arithmetic uses nearest-even rounding. The supported whole-hour products are exact, so the two rounding rules do not conflict. Preserve signed integer division and MOD, which retains the sign of its first operand. Each multiplication, sum, subtraction and division rounds separately.

1. Round PTSTEP to ITIME. Compute IZT by rounding PTSTEP times the separately rounded sum of REAL(KSTEP) and one half. Store RSTATI as REAL(IZT). Store NSTADD as IZT divided by the rounded day and NSTASS as its remainder. Form the separate daily-update remainder from IZT plus half a day minus NSSSSS. Store RTIMTR as RTIMST plus RSTATI. Store RHGMT as the remainder of rounded RSTATI plus NSSSSS, converted to FP64.
2. Evaluate the frozen orbital stage at RTIMTR. Store distance RDEASO, declination RDECLI and clock correction REQTIM. Store RSOVR as REQTIM plus RHGMT. Form RWSOVR by multiplying RSOVR by two, then by RPI, then dividing by RDAY. Select ZI0 from the original RSOLINC. Form ZSEASON as the separately rounded square of REA divided by the separately rounded square of RDEASO. Store RIP0 as ZI0 times ZSEASON and copy it to RII0. Store the sine and cosine of declination and hour angle using the qualified exact helper entries.
3. Form RDTSA as one half times PTDT, divided by RA. Store its separately rounded square as RDTSA2. Store that square divided by six as RDTS62 and divided by two as RDTS22. Store PTDT as RTDT and the original maximum of PTDT and one second as TSPHY.
4. Recompute ITIME and IZT from stored TSTEP in the same order as step one. Form radiation ZSTATI as REAL(IZT) plus the left-associated product of one half, the integer difference NRADFR minus one, and ITIME. Retain those rounded operations even when the product is zero. Replace the daily-update remainder with MOD of IZT and the rounded day. Set ZTIMTR to RTIMST plus ZSTATI and ZHGMT to the remainder of rounded ZSTATI plus NSSSSS.
5. Call the complete accepted direct UPDRGAS child with the original DYNA, ERAD, ERDI and RIP objects, and absent PI05. Re-read the changed RSOLINC. Recompute RIP0 and RII0 with the already rounded ZSEASON. Set the local greenhouse update indicator to one.
6. Evaluate the orbital stage at ZTIMTR. Store the radiation hour correction and angle, followed by their sine and cosine and the sine and cosine of radiation declination, in the six ERIP fields. The selected radiation frequency updates these values on every call.
7. Form ZANGOZC as the separately rounded sum 1.7535 plus 6.283076 times the first orbital theta, followed by subtraction of 1.7535. Call complete direct SUECOZO with that angle and the original ozone object. Form IMINUT by rounding the sum ZSTATI plus REAL(NSSSSS), divided by sixty. Call complete direct UPDECOZC, then UPD_GHGCLIM, then UPDECAEC, with the original argument order. With LPHYLIN false, the original aerosol condition is true for either value or absence of the optional aerosol flag. Each child owns every selected numerical stage.
8. At the original daily-update condition, with non-negative KSTEP, call UPDRGAS a second time. Preserve its position after the monthly updates. No later RIP0 recomputation is added.

Formatted diagnostic output and profiling hooks do not define the canonical numerical state. Their original text and profiling counts are outside this contract. The source reference remains separate and retains its ordinary diagnostics. No driver or source call supplies an omitted calculation in the direct entry.

## Bounds, Status And Acceptance

The direct entry uses a fixed, 16-byte-aligned stack frame. It preserves x18, x19 through x29, the return address and the lower 64 bits of v8 through v15. The entry requires aligned, non-overlapping complete argument owners and the accepted descriptor bounds. Optional pointer presence is tested before dereference. Unsupported configuration, invalid input or insufficient storage fails before writes.
