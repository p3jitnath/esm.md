## Complete Numerical Update

The first pass updates daylight layers at or below the supplied tropopause. The second pass updates daylight layers above it. For a lower layer, multiply the fixed ratio by oxygen and add water with separate FP64 rounding. Divide water by this combined column, then apply the supplied fraction cap. Multiply by eight, truncate towards zero for the interval index and subtract the converted integer for the nonnegative MOD fraction. This preserves signed zero.

The lower table has nine mixture entries, five temperature entries and thirteen pressure entries, so its flattened row count is 585 per stored spectral point. With one-based source indices $`J_P,J_T,J_{T1}`$ and mixture interval $`j`$, the first rows are $`I_0=[5(J_P-1)+(J_T-1)]9+j`$ and $`I_1=[5J_P+(J_{T1}-1)]9+j`$. Form each four-product sum in coefficient-first order with weights 00, 10, 01 and 11, retaining a rounded addition after each product. Blend the adjacent mixture sums with separately rounded one minus the mixture fraction and the fraction itself, then multiply by the combined column.

After multiplying the combined column by its interpolated line coefficient, multiply ozone by the lower ozone coefficient and add it. For each lower continuum, subtract adjacent temperature coefficients, multiply the difference by its fraction and add the first coefficient. Multiply by its density factor, add self then foreign terms, multiply by water and add this result to the already rounded line-plus-ozone depth. The upper branch has 235 rows per spectral point. Its zero-based rows are $`I_0=5(J_P-13)+(J_T-1)`$ and $`I_1=5(J_P-12)+(J_{T1}-1)`$. Multiply their sum by oxygen, then multiply ozone by the upper ozone coefficient and add it. The upper branch must not read water or continuum values.

For lower Rayleigh scattering, subtract adjacent mixture coefficients, multiply by the mixture fraction and add the first coefficient. Multiply the result by the total molecular column. The upper branch multiplies this column by its spectral upper coefficient. Write scattering separately from absorption.

The solar-layer selector starts at each column's tropopause. A lower-layer pressure index below the frozen reference followed by a next-layer index at or above it replaces the selector with the smaller of the next layer and the tropopause. Updates follow the original chronological loop order. At the currently selected lower layer, interpolate the solar flux between adjacent mixture entries with subtraction, multiplication and addition.
