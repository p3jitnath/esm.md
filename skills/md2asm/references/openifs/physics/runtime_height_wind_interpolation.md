# Runtime Wind at a Specified Height

This qualifies the unchanged direct [VDFVINT body](../../../../../src/openifs/physics/height_wind_interpolation.S) as a runtime CALLPAR component. Its inputs are wind and geopotential at the full model levels and a requested height of 100 or 200 m. Geopotential has units J/kg, wind has units m/s, and gravity has units m/s². The dimensionless planet factor is one from the actual dynamical producer. The target geopotential is the height multiplied by the separately rounded ratio of gravity to that factor.

For each selected column, the source scans adjacent levels from the bottom upward and selects the first bracket. The lower geopotential endpoint is included, and the upper endpoint is excluded. Each returned wind is the weighted linear average of the two supplied winds, using their geopotential distances from the target. The two products, addition and division round separately. A column with no bracket keeps its complete incoming output word. An empty interval still calculates the source ratio and height product before return. No fused or approximate operation is added.

The original application binary interface, or ABI, keeps ten addresses. Registers x0 through x7 supply the first column, last column, column count P, level count L, eastward wind, northward wind, geopotential and scalar height. Incoming stack bytes zero and eight supply the two returned P-word wind vectors. Scalar integers occupy four bytes, and scalar height occupies eight bytes. Full fields hold P times L FP64 words in column-major order. All complete owners are mapped and naturally aligned. The mutable output owners are disjoint from each other and every input. Consumed field values are finite, and gravity and the planet factor are positive.

The direct body admits 1 through 4096 columns and 2 through 512 levels. Qualification includes 17/137 and 8/7, plus the column and level boundaries at 4096/2 and 1/512. Selected one-based columns lie within the supplied owner, and an inverted interval is empty. The 512-byte aligned frame preserves x18, x19 through x29, the return address and the low 64 bits of d8 through d15. FPCR must be zero. Invalid native control bounds keep their existing fatal refusal before output writes; the original interface has no scalar return value.
