# Surface Physical Updates Contract

The [formulation](../../../../../formulations/openifs/surface/surface_physical_updates.md) groups SURFEXCDRIVER_CTL_MOD::SURFEXCDRIVER_CTL, VSURF_MOD::VSURF and SURFTSTP_CTL_MOD::SURFTSTP_CTL from their same-named `surf/module/` sources in OpenIFS 48.1.1. The numerical methods and accepted child arithmetic remain frozen. The retained enclosing proofs and passing joint forecast gate admit these selected routes.

SURFEXCDRIVER_CTL prepares roughness, calls VSURF and carbon exchange, then retains skin conductivity, exchange coefficients, evaporation and surface energy in their source order. Its contained COMPUTE_DDH calculation remains in the same body. The current R5 controller corrects seven typed child addresses only: the five VUPDZ0 explicit vectors and the SRFCOTWO vegetation and liquid-water arrays. Their physical first addresses retain the signed descriptor offset and all lower-bound times stride terms. Caller descriptors remain unchanged. The retained complete caller proof and joint forecast gate pass for this correction.

VSURF prepares every selected tile and soil field and calls the full canopy-water-stress child. Its sea-R2 route applies the supplied salt factor to the complete saturation-temperature derivative. Retain nine tiles, four soil layers, the source legacy-resistance branch and the selected C3/C4 leaf route; the urban and alternative leaf models remain outside this scope.

SURFTSTP_CTL forms trial snow, interception, soil, sea-ice and lake states through direct children, then adds each trial-minus-old change divided by the supplied physics interval to its incoming rate. Retain clipping, flux signs, source reduction order, bottom drainage, multilayer snow and vegetation updates. Permanent-snow resets retain their explicit imposed mass boundary. Lake partial-profile revision 3 preserves inactive output bytes and calls the corrected complete lake child. These numerical buffers are unchanged by the separate SURFTSTP_LAYER descriptor overlay. The selected-range revision removes the stale full-column lake restriction while retaining all other route checks; its [controller contract](surface_time_update_controller.md) gives the complete interface.

The application binary interface, or ABI, retains 134 ordinary addresses plus one hidden character length for SURFEXCDRIVER_CTL, 51 addresses without a hidden length for VSURF, and 163 addresses without a hidden length for SURFTSTP_CTL. The first eight addresses use x0 through x7; the remaining addresses and any final eight-byte length use consecutive stack slots. Frames have 16,384, 4,096 and 12,288 bytes. Arrays retain their typed GNU descriptors, column-first strides, complete bounds and aliases. PMU0 is the explicit raw column vector at the SURFTSTP_CTL interface and receives its declared child view.

Preserve complete owners, readonly record targets and source-defined inactive bytes. Each frame has 16-byte alignment and preserves x18, x19 through x29, the return address and required low halves of v8 through v15. FPCR must be zero, with separately rounded FP64, nearest-even rounding, gradual underflow and signed zeros. Fused operations, contraction, reassociation and approximate arithmetic are forbidden. Original profiling, readonly surface constants, selected metadata lookup and accepted mathematical-helper services remain declared dependencies.

The source-descriptor layer overlay is outside these three routine bodies. Its 62 whole descriptors, 59 sections and 20 local-buffer descriptors retain their separate sealed representation receipt and enclosing caller comparison. Existing child science, full and partial complete-owner, raw-status and ABI proofs combine with stage completion and the passing joint final-state and saved-file gate. The separate layer overlay adds no routine credit. Build each self-contained source with `gcc -c -fPIC -march=armv8.2-a+simd` and retain the current private entries and direct child bindings. No model, science rerun, autonomy or performance result is added.
