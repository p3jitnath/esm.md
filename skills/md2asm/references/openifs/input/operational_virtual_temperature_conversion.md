# Operational Virtual Temperature Conversion

Virtual temperature is the temperature that dry air would need to have the same density as moist air at the same pressure. This initial SPECRT stage preserves the [physical formulation](../../../../../formulations/openifs/dynamics/virtual_temperature_conversion.md) and [mixture calculation](../dynamics/gprcp.md). At each active cell, multiply temperature in kelvin by the mixture gas constant, round once, then divide by the dry-air gas constant and round again. Every operation uses FP64, the 64-bit floating-point format, with separate rounding and no fused multiply-add.

The entry `md2asm_initialize_operational_spectral_virtual_temperature(G,TGFL,YGFL,SP,constants)` takes five addresses in x0 through x4. Their complete record sizes are 20,400, 1,096, 1,359,136, 2,992 and 520 bytes. Geometry G describes the grid, TGFL describes moisture storage, YGFL describes the nine physical fields, and SP describes spectral coefficients. These records and their declared backing arrays are mapped, aligned to eight bytes, nonwrapping and disjoint except for their documented borrowed views. Their owners remain live through return.

The selected grid has 137 layers and 1,649,920 local points in 97,055 blocks of 17. The final block has two active points. Live level maps assign 69 or 68 layers to each spectral process group. The parent uses public inverse and direct transform interfaces around the ordered grid conversion. It owns a 1,808,328,760-byte grid temporary and an 18,632-byte mixture temporary, then releases both. Only spectral temperature changes. Vorticity, divergence, surface coefficients, moisture and metadata remain unchanged.

The direct helper `md2asm_operational_hydrostatic_private_gprcp` keeps the original sixteen GPRCP arguments and appends a borrowed 64-byte dependency record as argument seventeen, at incoming stack byte 64. Its words at bytes 0 and 24 hold the constants and YGFL addresses. The remaining words are unused by this helper. Its frame is 192 bytes. It uses the original active bounds and optional output rules from the mixture contract, with no original OpenIFS global inputs.

The parent has a 512-byte aligned frame and preserves the Arm application binary interface, including x18, saved registers and floating-point control FPCR. FPCR must be zero. Return 0 means success, 1 means temporary allocation failure, and 2 means refusal before allocation. Natural arithmetic flags and the system error value remain visible. Cleanup releases partial temporary storage.

All four process maps pass complete spectral-owner comparison, with zero differing bytes. Borrowed metadata and input coefficients remain unchanged. Independent checks cover full and final blocks, the ideal-gas relation and unchanged inactive rows. Local records retain status, buffer guards and allocation-failure checks. Complete initialisation and forecast acceptance remain separate.
