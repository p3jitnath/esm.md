# Convective Downdraft Descent on the GPU

Revision 1 implements complete CUDDRAFN from [downdraft physics](../../../../../formulations/openifs/physics/convective_downdraft_descent.md), preserving the [ordered CPU contract](../../../../md2asm/references/openifs/physics/cuddrafn.md). A downdraft is a descending parcel of air. Its downward mass flux is negative. Air entering and leaving that parcel changes its energy, water and rain transport.

Visit source levels from three to the bottom. Continue a column only when its downdraft flag is set and its preceding downward mass flux is negative. Retain each source phase in order, including pressure masks, mixing, transported energy and water, bounded temperature, parcel adjustment, rain-limited stopping, rebuilt fluxes and kinetic energy. Run the complete CUADJTQ mode-two adjustment on the device. Keep source caps, comparison ties, inactive histories and every separately rounded FP64 operation. Upper-region detrainment, which describes air leaving the parcel, retains the source's complete selected-row writes.

The selected alias is `md2asm_cuddrafn`. Its 24 original addresses retain the order in the CPU contract and [host assembly](../../../../../src/openifs/physics/gpu/convective_downdraft_descent_calls.S). The domain has 1–4,096 allocated columns, 1–1,024 levels and a possibly empty selected interval. Logical flags are zero or one. The normal-physics flag is false, and all source coefficients, pressure differences, denominators and consumed values satisfy the qualified finite child domains. Unused ghosts, inactive histories and the unconsumed saturation field retain their exact words. Real arrays use column-first FP64 storage. Scalar controls require four-byte alignment and real owners eight.

[The complete device entry](../../../../../src/openifs/physics/gpu/convective_downdraft_descent.ptx) takes the original vector and common 64-byte context. The 192-byte record contains the adjustment child's first 144 constant-address bytes, heat capacity and gravity addresses at 144 and 152, an optional EXP counter owner and capacity at 160 and 168, and the live LHOOK address and capacity at 176 and 184. LHOOK is zero in this scope. Use one block, one thread and no dynamic shared memory. The 4,096-byte Arm frame preserves x18, saved registers, aligned stack, FPCR, inherited FPSR and natural errno across storage services.

Private scratch holds a header, column histories, a logical mask and ten complete output copies. With P columns and N levels, it needs `4096 + align8(52*P) + 8*P*(1+9*N)` bytes, where align8 rounds a byte count up to a multiple of eight. Check every complete span, integer product and forbidden overlap before numerical work. Commit all complete mutable owners only after success. The 40-byte pure result contains flags at 0, error at 4, the last errno event at 8, actual EXP calls at 16 and reserved zeros at 24 and 32. Counters count executed source calls and preserve wraparound.

Seventy-eight protected GPU calls cover 39 original cases and match 1,080,288 output bytes, with 45,450 independent physical relations. Fifty-five refusals, three ordinary-device graphs, 5,549 new actual EXP-word checks, native inspection and two source peers pass. Empty work follows the selected CPU early return, whose status can differ from original unconditional setup. Private CPU-parallel and other original-name aliases remain outside this packet. Complete CUMASTRN and GPU forecast acceptance remain pending.
