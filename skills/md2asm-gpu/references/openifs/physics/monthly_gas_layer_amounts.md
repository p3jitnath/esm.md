# Monthly Gas Layer Amounts on the GPU

Revision 1 implements the complete contained UPD_GHGCLIM::INTERP_GAS calculation from [monthly gas physics](../../../../../formulations/openifs/physics/monthly_gas_climatology.md), preserving the [ordered CPU contract](../../../../md2asm/references/openifs/physics/climatology_layer_mass.md). A climatology is an average over many years. This child blends two monthly volume fractions and converts their pressure-weighted amount to Pa. Dividing that amount by gravitational acceleration gives gas mass per square metre.

For each increasing pressure layer, subtract its top pressure from its bottom pressure, then multiply by the scaling factor. Reuse that separately rounded factor across the increasing latitude loop. At each latitude, subtract month two from month one, multiply by the interpolation weight, add month two and multiply by the layer factor. Every FP64 operation rounds separately. Preserve signed zeros, gradual underflow and the original source loop order.

The seven original addresses name month one, month two, weight, pressure descriptor, scaling, volume descriptor and returned-amount descriptor. Months are four-byte integers from 1 to 12. Real scalars and array cells use eight bytes. The one-, three- and two-dimensional GNU descriptors occupy 64, 112 and 88 bytes. Their bases address the first logical element, while signed strides and gaps give the stored layout. Support 0–4,096 latitudes and 0–512 layers with twelve supplied months. Volume and output extents match. A zero layer count returns before the latitude count or numerical inputs are read. Zero latitudes also return before numerical inputs.

Consumed weight lies from zero to one. Scaling and volume fractions are finite and nonnegative, including either zero sign. Pressure is finite and nondecreasing, and all intermediate values remain finite in the declared domain. Check complete descriptor spans against their supplied owners, then inspect only values consumed by the two selected months. Preserve all input bytes, unused months and output gaps.

[The device entry](../../../../../src/openifs/physics/gpu/monthly_layer_amount_entry.ptx) takes the original seven-address vector and common 64-byte context. Its 48-byte record stores complete pressure, volume and output owner bases and byte counts. No scratch is required. Check every address, signed product and forbidden mutable overlap before numerical stores. Descriptors, real values and metadata require eight-byte alignment, while month integers require four. Use one block, one thread and no dynamic shared memory. The 32-byte pure return contains flags at 0, error at 4, event zero at 8, one completed-call observation at 16 and reserved zero at 24. This observation is separate from production counters.

[Host assembly](../../../../../src/openifs/physics/gpu/monthly_layer_amount_calls.S) retains `md2asm_climatology_layer`, computes storage bounds without numerical work and queries actual serial state. Its 640-byte frame preserves x18, saved registers, 16-byte stack alignment, FPCR, inherited FPSR and errno. The contained Fortran reference retains its parent-owned month bindings through the accepted source bridge, while the direct child passes their identical values explicitly.

One hundred original, selected-CPU and GPU calls match 480,064 complete output bytes, with 114,424 independent 100-digit relations. They cover month boundaries, empty and maximum shapes, signed strides, gaps, signed zero and subnormal values. Thirty-two refusals, three source-unused success routes, a fresh-status ordinary-device graph with fourteen address relocations, native inspection, current provider identity and an independent complete source peer pass.
