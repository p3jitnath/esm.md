RN means one FP64 rounding to nearest with ties to even, gradual underflow, and preserved signed zero. In a daylight lower cell compute C=RN(water+RN(STRRAT*oxygen)), p=RN(water/C), and replace p with cap only when p>=cap. Compute x=RN(8*p), J=INT(x) with truncation towards zero, and f=RN(x-FP64(J)). The original MOD(x,1) has these exact bits on 0<=x<8, including signed zero. Preserve conversion IXC when x differs from its exact integer round trip. Lower i0=9*(5*(JP-1)+JT-1)+J and i1=9*(5*JP+JT1-1)+J address the 585-row table. Form L by adding table[i0]*FAC00, table[i0+9]*FAC10, table[i1]*FAC01, and table[i1+9]*FAC11 from left to right. Form U at those same indices plus one. Then A=RN(C*RN(RN(RN(1-f)*L)+RN(f*U))). Preserve the table as the first operand of each lower product.

Each continuum uses D=RN(density*RN(b0+RN(fraction*RN(b1-b0)))). First add RN(ozone*ABSO3AC[g]) to A; then add RN(water*RN(Dself+Dforeign))). Self rows use pitch 10 and adjacent index INDSELF; foreign rows use pitch 3 and adjacent INDFOR. In an upper cell the 235-row, one-species table uses i0=5*(JP-13)+JT-1 and i1=5*(JP-12)+JT1-1. Add FAC00*table[i0], FAC10*table[i0+1], FAC01*table[i1], and FAC11*table[i1+1] from left to right, then multiply by oxygen and add RN(ozone*ABSO3BC[g]). Preserve factors as the first upper operands. Upper cells do not read water, cap, or continuum payloads. At the start of each lower spectral point interpolate ray=RN(r0+RN(f*RN(r1-r0))) from RAYLAC[g+16J] and its adjacent node, then scattering=RN(total molecules*ray). At each upper point scattering=RN(total molecules*RAYLBC[g]). Preserve Rayleigh preparation before the point absorption. There is no scalar Rayleigh owner.

Complete the lower pass before the upper pass, each in increasing layer then column order. Each column has its own private solar-selection integer, initially equal to its tropopause. In each lower cell read both current JP and the next stored level JP. If current JP<LAYREFFR and next JP>=LAYREFFR, replace that column's selection by min(current one-based layer+1,tropopause). At the selected lower layer interpolate solar=RN(s0+RN(f*RN(s1-s0))) from adjacent nine-node solar columns J and J+1. Store gas, conditionally solar, then Rayleigh at every one of eight points. Retain repeated writes and the last actual write. Upper cells change no solar state. A forecast may consume only solar words defined by this source route. Private per-column integers use 16384 bytes of bounded thread-local storage for at most4096 columns; they are never an original model owner.
