# Complete Ordered Radiation Field Reduction on the GPU

The internal `md2asm_reduce_longwave_field` call combines absorption samples into groups. The longwave and shortwave parents supply 186 field plans. The GPU performs a complete field in one launch and retains the equations and units in the [longwave](../../../../md2fort/references/openifs/radiation/longwave_gpoint_reduction.md) and [shortwave](../../../../md2fort/references/openifs/radiation/shortwave_absorption_setup.md) contracts. FP64 means 64-bit floating-point arithmetic.

The Arm application binary interface, or ABI, uses `x0` for source, `x1` for destination, `x2` for positive 32-bit group counts, `x3` for group count, `x4` for weights or zero and `x5` for five 64-bit shape words. These words give cell count and source-cell, source-term, destination-cell and destination-group strides in bytes. A stride is the distance between neighbouring stored values. Original parents provide complete mapped spans with valid bounds. FP64 spans require eight-byte alignment and counts require four-byte alignment. The selected plans contain at most 1,175 cells, sixteen groups and sixteen terms. The internal call has no declared return value.

The parallel route gives one complete cell to each thread, using 128 threads per block and enough blocks to cover all cells. Extra threads return before numerical loads. Each thread visits its groups and terms in their original order. A group sum starts at positive zero. Each weighted term has a separately rounded multiplication followed by addition through the unchanged IEEE helpers. Counts are reread for each cell and weights for each term. Nonfinite values retain their original arithmetic and flags. No sum is split, fused or reassociated.

The host chooses parallel work only when different cells cannot affect each other. Destination storage must be separate from source, counts and present weights. Source and other read-only views may share storage. All four array strides must be divisible by eight, with at least eight bytes between destination cells. Complete cell intervals or complete group intervals must be separate, which permits both cell-major and group-major layouts. Other valid layouts, dependent aliases, single-cell calls and unsupported parallel grid sizes use the unchanged serial entry. These are routing choices and do not add a public refusal. The original five shape words are copied before any store, so their original storage may overlap output.

The private vector has 96 bytes. It contains source, destination, counts and optional weights at bytes zero, eight, sixteen and twenty-four, group count at thirty-two, the five shape words at forty through seventy-nine and two zero words. The 64-byte context has error at zero, flags at eight, event at sixteen and a capacity-record address and length at forty-eight and fifty-six. The 64-byte capacity record holds vector, context, source, destination, count and optional-weight extents, followed by two zero words. Extents include holes between stored elements, whose bytes remain unchanged. These three private buffers are mutually disjoint and separate from model spans. No shared GPU memory or block barrier is needed.

The host retains its aligned 1,536-byte frame and preserves `x18`, saved registers, full vector registers, FPCR and errno. Newly raised flags join incoming FPSR through an integer bitwise OR. The complete event word and actual FAST and WIDE counters retain their original effects. Nonzero FPCR and an active OpenMP parallel region retain their original refusals. Only the unique full-grid origin writes a malformed-launch error.

The [Arm host](../../../../../src/openifs/radiation/gpu/longwave_ordered_reduction.S) selects the [parallel cell entry](../../../../../src/openifs/radiation/gpu/longwave_ordered_field_parallel_cells.ptx) or the unchanged [serial entry](../../../../../src/openifs/radiation/gpu/longwave_ordered_field_entry.ptx). Keep both entries and the shared IEEE-status and scalar-arithmetic providers once. The serial route preserves dependencies when model buffers share written storage.

The changed component passes all 186 retained fields, eighteen alias and status controls and twenty-three complete raw images, with 9,200,896 compared bytes and zero differences. These checks include multiple blocks, nonfinite values, refusal before writes, complete holes, preserved registers and actual counter wrap. The unchanged independent weighted-sum and shortwave physical checks remain applicable. Source pins, exact records and detailed tuning stay local. These component results do not establish complete forecast acceptance or a comparative runtime.
