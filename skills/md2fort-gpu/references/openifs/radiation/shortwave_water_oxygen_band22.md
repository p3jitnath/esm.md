Layer arrays have B*V values, cap and cosine B FP64 words, tropopause B INTEGER4 words, solar 16*B FP64 words, and each optical output 16*B*V FP64 words. The 80-byte live table record contains ABSA 74880 bytes, ABSB 30080, SELFREFC 1280, FORREFC 384, SFLUXREFC 1152, RAYL 8, LAYREFFR 4, STRRAT 8, NSPA 56, and NSPB 56, in that order. ABSA and ABSB use the actual yoesrta22.eq.1_ and yoesrta22.eq.0_ owners. The other band symbols use __yoesrta22_MOD_ and the two species owners use __yoesrtwn_MOD_. Band 22's species counts at byte twenty-four are 9 and 1. Two active points use the original 16-point storage. Live input tables retain their permitted variation.

RN means one FP64 rounding to nearest with ties to even, gradual underflow, and preserved signed zero. In a daylight lower cell compute C=RN(water+RN(RN(1.6*STRRAT)*oxygen)), p=RN(water/C), and replace p with cap only when p>=cap. Compute x=RN(8*p), J=INT(x) with truncation towards zero, and f=RN(x-FP64(J)). The original MOD(x,1) has these exact bits on 0<=x<8, including signed zero. Preserve conversion IXC when x differs from its exact integer round trip. Lower i0=9*(5*(JP-1)+JT-1)+J and i1=9*(5*JP+JT1-1)+J address the 585-row table. Form L by adding table[i0]*FAC00, table[i0+9]*FAC10, table[i1]*FAC01, and table[i1+9]*FAC11 from left to right. Form U at those same indices plus one. Then A=RN(C*RN(RN(RN(1-f)*L)+RN(f*U))). Preserve the table as the first operand of each lower product.

Each continuum uses D=RN(density*RN(b0+RN(fraction*RN(b1-b0)))). The lower gas depth before oxygen continuum is RN(A+RN(water*RN(Dself+Dforeign))). Self rows use pitch 10 and adjacent index INDSELF; foreign rows use pitch 3 and adjacent INDFOR. In an upper cell the 235-row, one-species table uses i0=5*(JP-13)+JT-1 and i1=5*(JP-12)+JT1-1. Add FAC00*table[i0], FAC10*table[i0+1], FAC01*table[i1], and FAC11*table[i1+1] from left to right, then form RN(RN(oxygen*1.6)*line). Preserve factors as the first upper operands. Upper cells do not read water, cap, or continuum payloads. In both branches first form O2CONT=RN(RN(K*oxygen)/700), where K is the exact widening of source binary32 4.35E-4, word 0x3f3c8216c0000000. FP64 adjustment1.6 is word0x3ff999999999999a and exact700 is word0x4085e00000000000. Add O2CONT to the lower or upper line-plus-water result at each of two points. Compute RN(total molecules*RAYL) once per active cell before its point loop.

Complete the lower pass before the upper pass, each in increasing layer then column order. Each column has its own private solar-selection integer, initially equal to its tropopause. In each lower cell read both current JP and the next stored level JP. If current JP<LAYREFFR and next JP>=LAYREFFR, replace that column's selection by min(current one-based layer+1,tropopause).
