# Surface-Pressure Mass Correction

Surface pressure is the weight of air above a unit area, measured in pascals, written Pa. This proportional PFIXER route corrects its global mean after transport. Divide the old weighted mean by the predicted weighted mean, multiply each predicted pressure by that ratio, then store its natural logarithm. The source division, each FP64 product and the public log function keep their original order and rounding.

`md2asm_runtime_surface_pressure_mass_correction(G,TGMV,YGFL,TDYN,pressure0,pressure1,log_pressure,dependency)` uses x0 through x7. G is the live geometry20400 record. TGMV is the live grid-motion1560 record. YGFL and TDYN are the caller’s field and dynamics records. The three complete numerical owners stay mapped, disjoint from each other and all records, and aligned to eight bytes. Pressure0 is read-only. Pressure1 and log_pressure are updated only at physical points.

Read block width P, block count B and physical point count N from G bytes 5144, 5160 and 7376. P is 1 through 4096, while B and N are positive signed 32-bit values. Require P times B minus P below N and N at most P times B. Each pressure owner needs 8 times P times B bytes. Integer addressing uses 64-bit counts. Final-block padding remains unchanged. Input pressures, means and corrected values are finite and positive. The logarithm follows the source value expressed in Pa and has no unit.

The 32-byte dependency borrows partition4096 at 0 and configuration67616 at 8. Its unused HYD64 address at 16 may be null, and word 24 is zero. This pressure entry selects four processes. They initialise the public communication and transform libraries for the same live grid, and agree route admission before entry. The [norm entry](distributed_grid_point_norms.md) defines the complete transform descriptor and result interface.

The selected source route has TGMV.YT1.NDIMS and MSP equal to one, YGFL.LTRCMFMG at 280 and NMFDIAGLEV at 320 zero, and TDYN.NGPMASCOR at 4584 zero. These select proportional correction with diagnostics disabled. The caller selects source LHOOK false. Mapped record capacities, valid aliases and pressure-value bounds belong to the caller.

The entry owns one 16 times P times B-byte two-field pack. It copies physical old and predicted pressures in P, 2, B order, then calls the accepted distributed norm. It divides the two means once, updates each physical pressure and calls the same public log function. Private padding that the source never reads remains unwritten. The 384-byte frame preserves x18, x19 through x30 and 16-byte stack alignment. No saved vector register changes. FPCR is zero.

Return 0 on success, 1 if any process cannot allocate its pack, or 2 for unsupported input. Child refusal is propagated before pressure updates. An added integer collective agrees allocation and child status. Only this extra coordination restores its incoming numerical flags and system error value, leaving the original numerical calls’ natural state visible. Successful peers release their pack after a single-process allocation failure. Production calls only direct assembly and declared public libraries.

Four cases on each process cover uniform and varying pressure, equilibrium, 17-position blocks with two final physical cells, and 31-position blocks with seven final physical cells. All 422,383,936 complete pressure and log-pressure bytes plus 384 raw-status bytes match the unchanged Fortran routine exactly. Each implementation passes 99,001,680 independent positivity, proportional-change, input-preservation and sampled logarithm relations. Native ABI, 40 refusals, asymmetric allocation failure, padding, guards and tracked-owner release pass. The same assembly handles both layouts, and no Fortran main is rebuilt. Both layouts use the same 9 km grid, so this gives no second-resolution or complete forecast acceptance.
