# Pressure-Weighted Column Integral

A column integral adds the mass of a carried substance through the atmosphere. This GPPWC entry uses its mass fraction q in kg/kg, pressure p in Pa and gravity g in m/s². Each boundary value adds q times the layer pressure difference divided by g to the value above, giving kg/m². The top boundary is positive zero. The reciprocal, difference, two products and addition keep the source FP64 rounding order.

`md2asm_explicit_pressure_weighted_column_integral(KPROMA,KSTART,KPROF,KFLEV,PWCH,PQ,PRXP,HYD64)` passes eight addresses in x0 through x7. The first four point to 32-bit integers with four-byte alignment. P=KPROMA is 1 through 4096 columns and L=KFLEV is 0 through 4096 layers. KSTART is 1 through P+1 and KPROF is 0 through P. Their inclusive active interval may be empty. These are runtime counts, with no recompilation to change shape.

PWCH and PRXP store P by L+1 FP64 values in column-first order, requiring 8 × P × (L + 1) bytes each. PQ stores P by L values, requiring 8 times P times L bytes. All buffers align to eight bytes. Their complete ranges stay mapped and do not wrap. PWCH is disjoint from every input, including scalar and constant records. Read-only input views may overlap. Backing capacities and declared numerical bounds are caller obligations.

HYD64 is the borrowed 64-byte hydrostatic context. Its first word points to the accepted owned physical520 record, with RG at byte 112. Both records align to eight bytes and stay live through return. The entry loads gravity from this producer and uses no original OpenIFS global. The caller supplies the qualified positive finite constant and finite pressure and mass-fraction values.

The direct leaf uses the Arm application binary interface, or ABI, and has no frame, allocation or external call. It preserves x18, saved integer registers and saved vector registers. FPCR, the floating-point control register, is zero. The reciprocal is evaluated before an empty interval, retaining source numerical flags. Independent columns use two-lane vector instructions, with a scalar tail for an odd column. Every layer keeps its previous boundary value and exact update order. Inactive columns and all inputs remain unchanged.

Return 0 on success or 2 on unsupported dimensions, alignment, mode, wrapping or writable buffer overlap before output stores. Declared mapped scalar and constant bounds remain caller obligations. Twelve new source cases and twelve direct cases cover 17 columns with 137 layers, 8 columns with 5 layers, 31 columns with zero layers, final-two and shifted intervals, empty work and incoming status values 0 and 24. All 151,408 output bytes and 288 raw-status bytes match exactly. Both implementations pass 37,852 closed-form and inactive-column relations in total. ABI, three atomic refusals, inputs and guards pass. The original source object is unchanged and no Fortran main is rebuilt. Full transport and forecast acceptance remain separate.
