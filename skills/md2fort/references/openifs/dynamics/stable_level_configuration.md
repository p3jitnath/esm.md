# Stable Upper-Level Configuration

This source stage marks the upper model levels that use the existing stabilization scheme. A half level is a boundary between two model layers. The stage compares their pressures with the original threshold of 6,000 Pa and counts the selected levels. It preserves the physical model and the original conditional default.

The consumed source is `arpifs/setup/sudyn.F90`. LSETTLSVF is true when LECMWF and LSLAG are true, NSTOP is positive and NCONF is one of 1, 131, 302, 401, 501 or 801. It is calculated from these live inputs. RPRES_SETTLSVF is 6,000 Pa, RSCALE and RSCALEOFF retain the original dimensionless values of 1e14 and 1e-14, and LSETTLSVF_DIFF is false. NFLEVSF starts at zero. When LSETTLSVF is true, the source visits levels 1 through NFLEVG in order, counts each STPREH value no greater than the threshold, and limits the result to NFLEVG.

The C application binary interface is `int md2asm_initialize_stable_level_configuration(Geometry,geometry_capacity,MODEL,MODEL_capacity,platform16)`, with arguments in x0 through x4 and status in w0. Geometry has at least 17,096 readable bytes, MODEL has at least 4,275,088 readable and writable bytes, and both addresses have 8-byte alignment and complete mapped nonwrapping spans. The platform view has 16 readable bytes and 4-byte alignment. Its four int32 words are NCONF, LECMWF, LR2D and LR3D from the actual owned SUCT0 producer. The selected three-dimensional route requires LR2D zero and LR3D one. All logical inputs are canonical zero or one.

Geometry supplies NFLEVG at byte 5,224 and the owned STPREH rank-one descriptor at byte 3,272. Supported levels range from 1 through 255. An active pressure view has FP64 elements, unit stride, lower bound zero, upper bound NFLEVG and canonical origin. Its complete owner has NFLEVG plus one values. The stage reads only entries 1 through NFLEVG. Every consumed pressure is finite and nonnegative. An inactive branch does not inspect the pressure descriptor or payload. MODEL supplies NSTOP at byte 1,359,332 and LSLAG at byte 1,564,160. Borrowed inputs remain unchanged.

The writable MODEL fields are LSETTLSVF at byte 1,568,832, RPRES_SETTLSVF at 1,569,072, RSCALE at 1,569,088, RSCALEOFF at 1,569,096, LSETTLSVF_DIFF at 1,568,840 and NFLEVSF at 1,569,080. They contain 36 bytes in total. Return zero succeeds. Return two refuses an unsupported shape, invalid logical, invalid pressure or bounded address before mutation. All other MODEL bytes retain their incoming representation.

The entry is a leaf with no external call, no allocation and no stack frame. It preserves x18 and every saved register. FPCR must be zero. The pressure comparisons retain their original FP64 meaning, and constants are stored as their exact source bit patterns. The separate genuine source-segment reference and independent pressure-count relation must agree over the whole MODEL and borrowed inputs before live forecast integration. This is a consumed SUDYN stage, rather than a complete SUDYN result.

Seven genuine source-segment profiles pass, covering the selected 19-level shape, a pressure exactly at 6,000 Pa and its adjacent FP64 values, each inactive condition, and the supported 255-level limit. All 29,925,616 compared MODEL bytes agree, borrowed inputs remain unchanged, and fourteen independent condition and pressure-count relations pass. Eleven critical invalid-input cases return two before mutation. The native saved-register mask is zero, and floating-point status and errno match the source reference. These are explicit admissible pressure profiles, rather than a capture of the final live forecast pressure owner.
