# Distributed Spectral Norms

A spectral norm measures the size of a weather field from its coefficients. This numerical SPNORM component preserves the original sum order on the four-process grid. Each field keeps its physical units. The selected route has 137 layers, one thermal field, no spectral tracers and no terrain norm. Initial terrain loading does not determine the later terrain-norm switch. The consumed false value comes from SUGRIDG after loading.

`md2asm_compute_operational_distributed_spectral_norms(G,MODEL,SP,partition,controls,persistent,results)` uses x0 through x6. The first four complete records have 20,400, 4,275,088, 2,992 and 4,096 bytes. Controls has six 32-bit words, NSPPR, LELAM, LREFGEN, LREFOUT, NPROC and LDSPOR, with values 0, 0, 0, 0, 4 and 0. Persistent has three FP64 values, AVNRMDIV, AVNRMPD and AVNRMVD. Only AVNRMDIV changes, on the root process. Results has 5,615,952 bytes. All ranges and complete backing owners are mapped, aligned to eight bytes, nonwrapping and disjoint except declared read-only views. They stay live through return.

For each local wave, sum degrees in increasing order. Wave zero uses the real coefficient's square. Positive waves use separately rounded real and imaginary squares, their sum and the source factor of two. The scalar weight is one and the kinetic weight is minus the live RLAPIN value. Clamp each wave sum to at least 100 times the smallest normal FP64 value. Form the two kinetic matrices independently, add them and multiply by one half. Every operation rounds separately.

MPI, a public interface for communication between processes, copies the local matrices and integer maps. Direct assembly proves that each global level-by-wave position is covered exactly once. There is no floating-point collective reduction. Add waves 0 through 1279 in order, take a square root except for kinetic energy, then add the 137 level values in order and divide by 137. Pressure has one level. This retains the original SPNORMAVE order.

Results stores five means at byte 0, followed by 137 vorticity values at 40, divergence at 1136, energy at 2232, temperature at 3328 and one pressure value at 4424. Four 137-by-1280 matrices start at 4432 in vorticity, divergence, kinetic and thermal order. Nonroot results stay incoming. A 7,635,400-byte private workspace holds exact wire copies, kinetic scratch and map coverage. It is released on every returned path.

Return 0 on success, 1 on collective allocation failure, 2 on unsupported controls or invalid maps, and 3 on public MPI failure. All four processes call together after MPI initialisation. Use MPI_BYTE for field transport and integer MPI_MAX only for status. Preserve the Arm ABI, x18 and FPCR, with FPCR zero. Natural flags and system error values remain visible. The matrix helper has a 256-byte frame, and its ordered-average helper is a leaf.

All four maps match complete result and persistent bytes exactly against the genuine source numerical parent. Independent high-precision relations cover weights, placement and ordered means. Raw status, ABI, inputs, guards and collective failure/refusal paths pass. Original formatted diagnostics and complete forecast acceptance remain separate.
