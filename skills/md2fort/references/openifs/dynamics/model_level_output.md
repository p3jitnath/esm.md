# Model Level Output Contract

WRMLFP from arpifs/fullpos/wrmlfp.F90 selects and packs the requested model-level fields in their original order. The selected request contains temperature in kelvin, specific humidity in kilograms of water per kilogram of moist air, and logarithmic surface pressure. The direct spectral and grid packing children preserve the supplied field values and source output layout.

The Arm application binary interface passes 21 ordinary addresses and one final eight-byte hidden character length. The first eight addresses use x0 through x7, with the others and length on the incoming stack. CDCONF is the one-character selector at address argument 13 when numbering starts at zero. Its hidden length must equal one. The frame uses 2048 bytes with 16-byte alignment and preserves x18, x19 through x29 and the return address. The caller uses no numerical floating-point instruction; its children keep their accepted ABI.

The selected scope has one process and processor set, no separate input/output server, at most 39 requested fields, up to 506 spectral coefficients, up to 2048 grid points and a block pitch from 1 through 24. An array descriptor records the data address, bounds and strides. Present grid and spectral owners must cover the requested fields and complete declared extents, including the final partial block. The direct packing children receive these original descriptors and use their retained source-order copies. Request records and field maps keep their frozen local layouts. The caller allocates and releases its packed buffers through malloc and free.

The current guard accepts A, I and lowercase m. Original ALLFPOS passes m for model-level output, and original WRMLFP treats every selector other than I as the dynamics route. This revision adds only the integer comparison with ASCII value 109 and a branch to the existing dynamics path; all other guard and route restrictions remain. The selected m route reuses the same packing objects, numerical instructions and buffer bounds. It does not extend this contract to every possible original selector.

The original wroutspgb_ and wroutgpgb_ services still encode and write the packed files. These services and their metadata remain local runtime dependencies during routine verification. The complete encoding route is not autonomous and must be replaced before autonomous forecast acceptance. FPCR must be zero, with unchanged ordered FP64 arithmetic and output bits in the qualified children.

The retained source-control proof records the actual rejected m call and independent source review of the two added integer instructions. This publication copy changes headers only and performs no new build or scientific check. The retained enclosing-owner checks and passing joint final-state, immediate-status, native ABI and ten-file gate cover this selected guard correction. This guard correction gives no separate routine credit.
