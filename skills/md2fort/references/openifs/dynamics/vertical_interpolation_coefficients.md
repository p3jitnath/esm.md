# Vertical Interpolation Coefficients

This setup prepares the coefficients that interpolate fields between vertical levels. It follows the complete selected calculation in `arpifs/interpol/suvsleta.F90`. The existing vertical and standard-atmosphere producers own its input coordinates and pressures. The numerical forecast children remain unchanged.

The frozen C interface is `int md2asm_initialize_vertical_interpolation_coefficients(Geometry,geometry_capacity,MODEL,model_capacity)`. Geometry and MODEL arrive in x0 and x2, with their byte capacities in x1 and x3. They are aligned, mapped, disjoint, non-wrapping records of at least 17,096 and 4,275,088 bytes. The supported level count is 4 through 255. The separate `md2asm_release_vertical_interpolation_coefficients(MODEL,model_capacity)` releases all owners in reverse source allocation order.

The original source owns 12 arrays and the derived lookup dimension NRLEVX and its FP64 value VRLEVX. It allocates four full-level coefficient arrays, computes their selected coefficients, then does the same for four half-level arrays. Next it allocates and computes the vertical WENO combination coefficients, derives the source lookup dimension, allocates and fills each of the two level lookup maps, and allocates and fills the adjacent layer-spacing ratios. WENO is an interpolation method that combines nearby stencils while reducing the influence of a nonsmooth stencil.

All ordered FP64 differences, products, divisions, exponential calls, comparisons and source tie rules are frozen. Each source-unwritten boundary row retains its allocation bytes. The production entry uses the accepted direct assembly exponential primitive. It has no original OpenIFS numerical call or captured production data. Complete owner descriptors and derived scalar state publish only after successful completion. A failed allocation releases every temporary owner and leaves MODEL unchanged.

The live source controls select LSLHD false, LSLHDQUAD false, LHOISLT false, LSLTVWENO false and LRHSVWENO true. The vertical filter flag LSLVF may be false or true. When true, its mask and smoothly varying strength come from the live hybrid coefficient VBH, standard-atmosphere pressures and named filter constants. The current finite-element grid has LREGETA false, so the complete WENO coefficient calculation is required. Unsupported source branches refuse rather than silently substitute a different method.

For each full or half grid, four consecutive coordinate values determine the cubic denominator coefficients. The interpolation weights use these denominators to reproduce a cubic polynomial on those four coordinates. The source smoothing matrices and their identity branches retain their original operation order. The smallest adjacent half-level spacing sets the lookup resolution.

```math
NRLEVX=\operatorname{NINT}\left(\frac{2}{\min(1,\min_j(\eta_{j}-\eta_{j-1}))}+0.5\right)+1.
```

Here eta is the dimensionless vertical coordinate. NINT rounds to the nearest integer with ties away from zero. The source lookup loops increase their reference level by at most one at each lookup position, using their original greater-than-or-equal tie. The ratio array stores successive half-level spacing ratios, with its first element equal to one.

## Storage, ABI and Acceptance

The caller retains every borrowed input and its declared typed extent. VETAF has bounds zero through n+1, VETAH and VBH have bounds zero through n, STPREH has bounds zero through n and STPRE has bounds one through n. Eta coordinates are dimensionless, and pressure is in pascals. Consumed arrays are finite and contiguous, and both eta grids increase strictly. The filter constants are finite, with nonnegative strength and pressure boundary, a minimum factor from zero through one, and positive transition strength and pressure. Inactive filter inputs are not required. The derived NRLEVX must be at most 2,000,000, matching the complete interpolation child's supported lookup extent.

The coefficient arrays use the exact queried GNU Fortran descriptors. Full and half VCUICO have shape (4,n), VSLD have shape (3,n), and VSLDW and VSLVF have shape (3,3,n). Their last dimension starts at zero. GAMMA_WENO has shape (n,3) with unit lower bounds. The integer maps have bounds zero through NRLEVX, and VRDETAR has bounds one through n. Each descriptor offset is minus the sum of its lower bound multiplied by its element stride. Only the original source-written rows change. Three boundary rows in each cubic or smoothing owner and the source-skipped WENO rows retain their allocation preimages.

The parent frame is 512 bytes. It saves x18, all used callee-saved integer registers and the low 64 bits of d8 through d15. The stack stays aligned to 16 bytes. Release uses a 64-byte frame and clears owner base pointers. Return zero means success, one means allocation failure and two means an unsupported input. Every failure releases temporary owners and leaves MODEL unchanged. Release is safe after a failed setup and when repeated. The caller must release only owners created by this entry while MODEL remains mapped.
