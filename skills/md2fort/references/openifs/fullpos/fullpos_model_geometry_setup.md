# Model Fullpos User Geometry

This component owns the selected global MODEL path of SUFPMODELGEO and its numerical/storage children ALLOC_FPUSERGEO and CHECK_FPUSERGEO. It builds the user-grid record used by Fullpos from the same live Geometry supplied as both source and host. The exact selected grid has 32 Gaussian latitudes, 64 longitudes, spectral truncation 21, NPROMA 24, NHTYP zero and NSTTYP one. LDLAM and LELAM are false, KFPCONF and KFPBOYD are zero, the pole values are RMUCEN=1/RLOCEN=0 and RSTRET=1. Other Fullpos geometries and the complete SUBFPOS caller remain outside this checkpoint.

The genuine sources are arpifs/fullpos/sufpmodelgeo.F90 and arpifs/module/type_fpusergeo.F90. The typed query fixes all 53 TFPUSERGEO fields and the 30 consumed Geometry fields. TFPUSERGEO has 472 bytes. Its three 64-byte descriptors are NFPRGRI at byte 80, NFPMEN at 144 and FPMU at 208. The first two own 32 four-byte integers; FPMU owns 32 FP64 sine-latitude values. The source declaration defaults are owned by the separate direct entry md2asm_initialize_fullpos_user_geometry_defaults(x0=TFPUSERGEO472,x1=capacity), which returns zero or two and requires fresh null data words. It writes only declared scalar/character defaults and null allocatable data words, retaining all other preimages. This is distinct from any undocumented bytes in GNU's implicit allocation-constructor temporary. The full outer-array allocation in SUBFPOS is not claimed here.

The checked md2asm_initialize_selected_fullpos_model_geometry entry receives live Geometry17096 in x0, a complete 32-byte domain label in x1, controls24 in x2, caller-owned TFPUSERGEO472 in x3 and capacity in x4. Controls contain LDLAM, LELAM, KFPCONF and KFPBOYD as four 32-bit words at bytes 0 through 15, followed by live FP64 pi at byte 16. Pi is the accepted owned RPI at constants520+0; the source uses its equal YOMCST_IFSAUX::XRPI value. That original module remains reference-only. All controls select the values above, and pi is positive and finite. Every explicit view is eight-byte aligned, mapped, non-wrapping and pairwise disjoint. The NLOENG and NMENG integer owners each have 36 elements at logical indices -1 through 34, as allocated by SUGEM_NAML and SUGEM1A from NDGSAG=-1 and NDGENG=34. Their complete genuine GNU descriptors select the consumed slice 1 through 32 at data-base plus eight bytes, and their payloads are disjoint from writable output and explicit inputs. The three initial output data pointers are null. FPCR is zero. Source-unwritten Geometry bytes are live owned preimages, not constructed-state packet inputs.

The source first computes degrees_per_radian=180/pi and the four separate products for ELATC, ELONC, ELON0 and ELAT0. It copies all named dimensions, Gaussian-grid parameters and domain text, then allocates NFPRGRI, NFPMEN and FPMU in that order. ALLOC_FPUSERGEO fills the integer arrays with the largest 32-bit signed integer and the real array with the largest finite FP64 value. SUFPMODELGEO copies NLOENG into NFPRGRI, and CHECK_FPUSERGEO performs its complete selected global finalisation: the pole values become 1 and 0, LAM-only fields become their source zero/false/true values, the regular grid has NLON points on every latitude, truncations are clamped nonnegative and the source stretch validity subtraction is evaluated.

The record's model-spectral/grid/core flags, coordinate-change flag, biperiodic flag, map-factor flag and buffer-shape flag follow the genuine comparisons and source order. The three FP64 coordinate comparisons each subtract before absolute value and tolerance comparison. NFPSIZEG is the exact integer sum of NFPRGRI, and KFPCONF/KFPBOYD zero selects NFPSIZEG_DEP=NFPSIZEG. The source then copies resolution/spectral/local point counts and NMENG. Finally it queries PMU=sin(Gaussian latitude) from the already-established public ECTRANS resolution through the unchanged accepted md2asm_call_public_transform_inquiry. The 47-word argument table has KRESOL in slot 0 and a complete FP64 rank-one descriptor in slot 40; all other arguments are absent. The new descriptor output shape passes the whole genuine SUFPMODELGEO source gate, including every PMU value.

The four LAM-angle products are executed even though global CHECK_FPUSERGEO later overwrites their record fields. The producer receives their live bits and does not replace them with invented physical zeros. No operation reads undefined numerical owner bytes. The final caller-owned padding at bytes 37–39 and 468–471 remains unchanged; no uniform-record preimage is required.
