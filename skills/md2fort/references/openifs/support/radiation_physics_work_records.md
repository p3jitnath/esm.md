# Radiation Physics Work Records

These three services replace original `AUX_RAD_TYPE_INIT`, `AUX_RAD_TYPE_UPDATE_VIEW` and `AUX_RAD_TYPE_FINAL` from `ecphys_aux_rad_type_mod.F90`. They allocate or select storage without calculating a radiation or weather value. The raw interfaces, 192-byte frame with 16-byte stack alignment, original GNU class adapters, ownership rules, allocation failure cleanup and preserved status follow the checked auxiliary work-record boundary in [the auxiliary contract](auxiliary_physics_work_records.md).

The raw entries are `md2asm_initialize_radiation_work_record(record, geometry, variables, levels, persistent)`, `md2asm_update_radiation_work_views(record, block)` and `md2asm_finalize_radiation_work_record(record)`. The writable radiation record spans 2,664 bytes. Geometry and variable inputs retain their complete 17,096-byte and 72,696-byte original layouts, eight-byte alignment and disjoint mapped spans. The caller supplies nonnegative levels, positive row and block counts, a zero-or-one persistent flag and shape products that fit the complete mapped allocations. No owned field is live at initialization entry. All fields needed by a view update must remain live and meet the accepted original descriptor and block preconditions.

Initialization borrows eighteen original radiation-field pointers in source order. It allocates seven rank-two fields with shape `[rows, blocks]` and seven rank-three fields with shape `[rows, levels, blocks]`. Nonpersistent storage selects one block with the thread-buffer flag. Persistent storage retains all geometry blocks. New numerical bytes remain undefined until their producer writes them. Every public view and other incoming record byte is preserved.

Update associates all 32 public views in original source order. It uses the qualified rank-two leaf for one-dimensional views and unchanged accepted rank-three and rank-four leaves for larger fields. The borrowed aerosol field has rank four and six component planes in the verification input. This record does not remap any lower bound to zero. The pointer relationships, strides, bounds, complete defined headers and every numerical owner remain source-exact. Finalization frees only the fourteen owned fields and clears their field slots. Borrowed fields and public descriptors remain unchanged.

Individual typed layout queries establish each field slot, public view and source variable pointer. Three focused cases cover thread-local storage, the persistent last block and empty vertical layers. Nine raw or original-interface ABI calls preserve saved registers, `x18`, FPCR, FPSR and errno. These cases compare all source-defined owned descriptors, complete public view descriptors, unchanged record bytes and complete borrowed or produced numerical buffers. No original procedure supplies a production stage. Independent source review and the joint complete forecast passed, including the native counts and complete byte gate recorded in the auxiliary contract. Original metadata construction remains an explicit caller boundary.

The three original-interface entries also increment their own aligned 64-bit integer counters in INIT, UPDATE_VIEW and FINAL order. These private observations use only caller-saved integer registers and do not change a field, numerical flag or library error value. The joint gate reads these three words and retains the earlier state-view counter scope.
