# Distributed Spherical Laplacian

This boundary implements original setup/sulap.F90 after the live four-process transform and SUMP partition. A spectral degree describes a spherical wave. The direct owner obtains local wave numbers and coefficient positions from the same two public TRANS_INQ calls as the source, then builds its integer maps and Laplacian factors. It does not use a saved map in production.

The application binary interface is int md2asm_initialize_distributed_laplacian(Geometry17096, constants520, partition_owner4096). All three addresses are mapped, eight-byte aligned, and stay valid through the collective call. The owner must be the accepted live partition owner for this exact Geometry address and world four. The nine TLAP descriptors at Geometry+3656 are fresh zero storage. All ranks call the boundary together. Return zero means ready, one means allocation failure with acquired data pointers cleared, and two means a collective refusal before mutation. AAPCS64 saved registers and x18 remain unchanged; the stack is sixteen-byte aligned. Release md2asm_release_distributed_laplacian(Geometry) frees these nine owners before the partition owner is released.

The supported source dimensions are NSMAX=NCMAX=1279, NUMP=NUMCP=640, NSPEC=409920, and NSPEC2=819840. The physical radius is constants+104, exactly 6371229 metres. The source allocation order is MYMS, RLAPDI, RLAPIN, NASN0, NASM0, NASM0G, NSPZERO, NVALUE, then NSE0L after the two public inquiries. Eight initial allocation attempts precede a collective status agreement; the ninth allocation has its own agreement before further work. Every successful local owner remains live through the forecast. Their total is 3,325,488 bytes per rank. The complete verification process, including the transform, has a 32 GiB address-space limit per rank and uses four two-CPU lanes on the owned day node. No model fields are allocated.

The first public inquiry supplies KASM0, argument 11 when arguments are numbered from zero; the second supplies KMYMS, argument 10. Argument zero is the live resolution address at Geometry+5168. The unchanged public 47-address packer preserves all absent optional slots. Because NCMAX equals NSMAX, the original source takes these two inquiry calls and does not call INI_SPEC_DIST. Geometry+3720 is the resulting NASM0 descriptor and Geometry+3912 is MYMS. The accepted SUMP producer owns NUMPP at +5256, NPTRMS at +5384, NALLMS at +5448, NUMLL at +5832, NPTRLL at +5896 and NPSURF at +6024.

For wave number $`m`$ and spherical degree $`n`$, NASN0 is n*n+n+1, NSPZERO is 2*n+2 and NASM0G is the one-based prefix of 2*(1280-m). NSE0L is the zero-based local prefix of 1280-m in the public MYMS order. NVALUE stores each degree twice in that same local order. The Laplacian factor is -float64(n*(n+1))/(RA*RA); its inverse is -(RA*RA)/float64(n*(n+1)). Each multiplication and division rounds separately to FP64, with round to nearest, ties to even and gradual underflow. Degrees minus one and zero have positive-zero factors. Fused operations, reassociation and approximate division are forbidden. The eigenvalue arithmetic reuses the accepted serial body without changing its ordered operations.

Acceptance requires all nine complete payloads and the complete TLAP descriptor bytes to agree with whole original SULAP under corresponding owned addresses. It also checks natural FPCR, FPSR and errno, saved registers, input preservation, first and last allocation cleanup, collective shape refusal, final release and all new integer-map relations. The unchanged independent spherical eigenvalue check is reused. A saved source packet is verification evidence only. This boundary supplies no time-step calculation and does not establish a complete forecast.

The complete original and direct comparisons pass all 13,301,952 payload bytes and 2,304 descriptor bytes across four processes, with zero differences. Sixteen introduced cleanup and refusal calls pass. Natural FPCR, FPSR and errno are 0, 24 and 777, and the native ABI mask is zero.
