# Fresh Model Trajectory Metadata

MTRAJ is a 2,656-byte record that reserves places for trajectory fields. A trajectory stores model states for later use. MTRAJ contains the 1,560-byte grid-volume record TGMV followed by the 1,096-byte constituent record TGFL. It is separate from the [192-byte trajectory owner](runtime_empty_trajectory_state.md) and that owner's 512-byte time records. The selected forecast keeps trajectory saving inactive.

The assembly entry `md2asm_initialize_fresh_model_trajectory(record,capacity)` supplies the source-defined fresh state. Its application binary interface, or ABI, receives the record address in x0 and byte capacity in x1. The caller owns a mapped, eight-byte-aligned span of at least 2,656 bytes, with no live field allocation. The entry sets 17 eight-byte base addresses to null. Their offsets are 0, 136, 248, 384, 496, 632, 744, 880, 1,560, 1,696, 1,832, 1,968, 2,104, 2,240, 2,376, 2,512 and 2,648. The first 16 bases describe unallocated arrays. The last is the YGFL constituent pointer.

All other 2,520 bytes retain their incoming values. These include undefined scalar values and unused array-description words. An array description stores a field's address, shape and spacing. Those undefined words must remain unread until their source producer assigns them. Original MTRAJ_CREATE and MTRAJ_DELETE perform no field work when profiling is inactive, so no original constructor or model call is needed in production.

Return zero after the stores, or two before every store for a null address, invalid alignment, short capacity or wrapping span. This is fresh initialization and must not discard a live allocation. The caller retains the record through its final model-step reader. The assembly is a leaf routine, with no stack frame, allocation, service call or floating-point instruction. It preserves x18, all Arm callee-saved registers, SP, FPCR, FPSR and errno, the operating system error number.

One original metadata call and six native calls pass. The source comparison checks all 17 defined null bases before and after MTRAJ_CREATE without reading undefined words. Two complete native preimages preserve every other byte, and four invalid-input cases preserve the entire output and guards. Immediate ABI masks are zero, and FPCR, FPSR and errno retain their seeded values. All guarded mappings are released and the checked process has exited. This establishes fresh metadata for inactive saving. Active field storage and complete forecast agreement remain separate requirements. Detailed records and tools remain local under `.runtime/`.
