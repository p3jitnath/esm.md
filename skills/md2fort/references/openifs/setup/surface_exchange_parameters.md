# Selected Surface Exchange Parameters

Revision 1 owns the complete selected SUPHY1 parameter stage. This Meteo-France setup routine is called unconditionally by SUPHMF, including in the selected ECMWF forecast. Its TPHY1 record contains soil, snow, vegetation and near-surface exchange controls. Its TVDOZ record contains ozone diffusion and deposition controls. This component does not replace the distinct ECMWF SUSURF parameter family or the surface meteorological state constructor.

The application binary interface (ABI) is int md2asm_initialize_surface_exchange_parameters(text, bytes, constants, physical, ozone, physical_capacity, ozone_capacity). Registers x0 through x6 contain live configuration text, its byte length, the owned physical-constant record, writable TPHY1 and TVDOZ records, and their capacities. Text contains one through 262,144 bytes. The constant record occupies 520 bytes, TPHY1 occupies 1,696 bytes and TVDOZ occupies 15,864 bytes. Records have eight-byte alignment and complete mapped nonwrapping spans. The two outputs are disjoint from every input and each other. Immutable inputs may overlap. Output capacities meet their complete record sizes, and output records may contain any incoming bytes. FPCR must be zero. Return zero on success or two before mutation for an unsupported checked input.

The physical constants come from the accepted owned producer and its setup flag is one. The consumed values are pi in radians, the 86,400-second day, gravity in metres per second squared and ice heat capacity in joules per kilogram per kelvin, at constant offsets zero, forty, one hundred twelve and three hundred fifty-two. The first supported uppercase NAMPHY1 and NAMVDOZ groups must be empty. The bounded integer reader retains the qualified literal group matching, ten-column marker, whitespace, comment and slash rules. The selected LECMWF source branch has no assignments and therefore consumes no additional state.

Every declared member of both records receives its source-defined value. Five vegetation arrays contain eighteen entries each. GCZ0H contains four rows by four columns, SODELX contains ten entries indexed zero through nine, and the twenty ozone deposition arrays each contain ninety-nine entries. TPHY1 has eight source-unwritten padding bytes at 1,612 through 1,615 and 1,692 through 1,695; they remain incoming. TVDOZ has no source-unwritten member or padding byte. Fixed literal stores and their field offsets come only from the original declarations and SUPHY1 assignments, before any returned reference record is observed.

SODELX begins with one divided by the square root of one plus two times pi. Each later entry equals the previous entry times two, then times pi, with a separate FP64 rounding after each operation. XCRINR is one divided by gravity. GSNC1 is pi divided by the left-associated product of 2.22, ice heat capacity, day length and 1,000. These variable-input source calculations run in assembly in this order. Independent literal ratios retain the strict source constant folding: GWLEX is two divided by three, TOEXP is 0.24 divided by 86,400, TOLIN is 0.008 divided by 86,400 and VOZHS is one divided by 86,400. All other source literals retain their original FP64 or four-byte integer and logical representation.

Albedo and emissivity controls are dimensionless fractions. Reservoir depths and roughness lengths retain the original metre convention, temperatures retain kelvin and snow-albedo decay rates retain inverse seconds. The ozone day threshold is one watt per square metre, and its rain threshold is one kilogram per square metre per day expressed per second. Ozone diffusion and deposition remain false and all twenty inactive velocity tables receive the original minus-999 sentinel. Other empirical coefficients retain their declared source role and value; this ownership change does not assign new units where the original declaration does not provide them.

The constructor allocates no heap storage, has no original callable or module input and requires no release. It preserves x18, all saved registers, FPCR and errno while retaining all natural source arithmetic flags. It uses a sixty-four-byte, sixteen-byte-aligned frame. Integer literal-table helpers and the bounded text reader introduce no floating-point operation. No fused, reordered, approximate or reduced-precision arithmetic is used.

Acceptance requires complete genuine SUPHY1 output under distinct preimages, immediate ABI and status, all source padding and caller guards, bounded live-text rejection and protected-page checks. The original reference constructs its consumed constants through genuine SUDYNCORE and SETUP_CONSTANTS before the SUPHY1 boundary. Independent checks cover albedo and emissivity ranges, sea and ice temperatures, the soil recurrence, gravity inversion, snow decay rates and the disabled ozone tables. A pure assembly executable must produce every record byte without original libraries or prepared state. Reuse the accepted physical-constant evidence and run no forecast or timing for this setup stage.
