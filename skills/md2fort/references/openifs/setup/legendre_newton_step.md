# Legendre Newton Step

CPLEDN performs one step used to generate the Gaussian latitude grid. Gaussian latitudes are chosen so that weighted latitude sums integrate polynomials accurately. The IFS weights represent one half of the integral from minus one to one, so the complete weight sum is one. This follows the original normalized-polynomial convention, in which one half of the squared-polynomial integral is one. They are roots of the Legendre polynomial of degree 32 for the selected T21 grid. This child covers KN=32, KODD=0 and KFLAG zero or one. Its Fourier coefficient input PFN has 17 FP64 elements with indices zero through 16. The earlier coefficient-generation calculation remains a separate production dependency; a verification driver does not supply it to a complete production forecast.

At angle theta in radians, the normalized polynomial is `P=0.5*PFN(0)+sum(PFN(k)*cos(2*k*theta))`. Its angle derivative is `D=-sum(PFN(k)*(2*k)*sin(2*k*theta))`. The terms are accumulated in increasing k from one through 16. Flag zero returns the Newton correction `delta=-P/D` and new angle `theta+delta`, while leaving the weight untouched. Flag one returns the Gaussian weight `65/(D*D)`, while leaving the new angle and correction untouched. Each multiplication, addition, subtraction and division retains the original FP64 rounding point. The derivative evaluates `(PFN(k)*(2*k))*sin((2*k)*theta)`. A fused operation or reassociation is forbidden.
