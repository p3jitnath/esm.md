# Cloud optical setup from live owners

This entry supplies the SUCLOPN and SU_CLOP550 calls at SUPHEC lines 349 and 353 from the caller's Geometry and MODEL records. A record is a fixed-layout block of model data. The entry reuses the accepted runtime optical calculation for one through 256 global levels, six shortwave bands and fourteen allocated bands. It adds no equation or allocation.

The application binary interface is `int md2asm_initialize_runtime_cloud_optical_from_owners(G,Gbytes,M,Mbytes)` in registers x0 through x3. G and M require complete mapped spans of 20,400 and 4,275,088 bytes. Each base is nonzero and aligned to eight bytes, each span must not wrap, and the two spans and canonical 48-byte output must be separate. MODEL byte eight must point to G. The floating-point control register must be zero. LRAY and LRAYFM15 must be false, and the global level count comes from G byte 5,224. Local level count does not select this calculation.

The entry passes MODEL's TERAD at byte 1,608,728 and TESWRT at byte 1,610,768 to the unchanged child, using their complete 2,040-byte and 10,744-byte spans. It takes NSW and NTSW from the live TERAD fields. The canonical output is the existing assembly-owned `md2asm_owned_cloud_optical_550_parameters`. Its five original field aliases, RSA55, RSB55, RSC55, RSD55 and RFA55, use this same owner. Every later consumer must bind to that owner. The wrapper writes only the child's 16 TERAD bytes, 6,088 TESWRT bytes and 48 canonical bytes; it preserves every other MODEL and Geometry byte.

Return zero means success and two means refusal before writes. The entry preserves x18, the Arm callee-saved registers, stack alignment, floating-point control and errno. The child produces its natural floating-point status flags. The wrapper frame is 112 bytes and the maximum nested stack use is 336 bytes. The caller owns all records and keeps the shared namespace live through its final reader.

A bounded check used detached copies of saved rank-four Geometry and MODEL records with 137 global and 68 local levels. Only the MODEL-to-Geometry pointer was changed to address the copied Geometry; all other saved pointers were inert. Two complete successful outputs matched the previously accepted 137-layer result and preserved all other record bytes. Short MODEL capacity, a wrong Geometry association and enabled LRAY each refused without output changes. All five immediate register masks were zero, control and errno were preserved, and guarded mappings and the child process were released. The 33 saved binary files, including status and arguments, total 30,049,056 bytes. No original routine, owner producer, model or collective call was replayed.

This check qualifies the optical forwarding boundary. Complete SUPHEC still requires its preceding SUSURF, SULWN, SUSWN, RSUN, reference-temperature and Planck-coefficient producers in their original order. This entry does not supply those stages or claim a complete forecast.
