Spectral truncation is the largest retained wave index. On the selected empty NAMDIM_TRAJ group, all twelve output words equal the input TDIM%NSMAX. No physical equation changes.

These addresses must refer to separate complete four-byte words. The output-address table is at least eight-byte aligned and remains read-only. The supported wave index is zero through 8,191. The entry returns zero on success or two before any output change for unsupported inputs.
