# Surface Roughness Contract

The [physical note](../../../../../formulations/openifs/surface/surface_roughness.md) defines how wind and surface fluxes set roughness. This complete replacement of `VUPDZ0_MOD::VUPDZ0` updates momentum, heat and moisture roughness, buoyancy flux, dimensionless height and moisture resistance for eight or nine surface tiles. A tile is one surface type inside a grid cell. The tiles are open water, sea ice, wet skin, low vegetation, exposed snow, high vegetation, sheltered snow, bare soil and optional lake.

## Supported State

The column count is 1 to 512. The active inclusive interval can be empty. Every rank-two array has exactly that column count and eight or nine tiles. Array storage is contiguous FP64, and complete owners and immutable inputs do not overlap. Vegetation lookup tables include the actual vegetation indices, soil index zero and snow index twelve. Gravity, pressure, air and skin temperatures, gas constant, heat capacity, viscosity coefficients, the wind floor and the friction-velocity floor are positive. Geopotential is positive, moisture and Charnock coefficients are nonnegative, tile fractions are in zero to one, low and high vegetation fractions sum to at most one, and the high vegetation fraction is less than one. Consumed values and intermediate results must remain finite. The sea-state branch admits a zero wave coefficient and its exact zero-to-zero power convention.

Both current components must be present to subtract ocean currents. A single present component is ignored, as in the original. Initial step zero estimates neutral stress and sets heat and moisture fluxes to positive zero. Later steps retain incoming fluxes. The wave switches, single-column fixed-roughness switches and lake state are supported. Fixed momentum and heat roughness replace their complete arrays, including inactive columns. Moisture roughness retains its computed value.

This is the complete eight- or nine-tile scientific domain. Optional urban tiles are excluded because the original sheltered-snow calculation reuses the final column's urban fraction across columns. Enabled lake physics requires tile nine, because the original accesses that tile even when the supplied extent has only eight tiles. The native entry and paired adapter reject these unsupported settings before writes. The genuine forecast report records the tile count, lake switch, wave switches, fixed-roughness switches, current presence and initial-step state.

## Ordered Calculation

Every displayed operation uses separately rounded FP64 arithmetic. First calculate density from pressure, temperature and moisture, then calculate the current-relative squared wind with its floor and the snow fraction clipped to zero through one. Initial roughness uses the complete [neutral ocean child](neutral_ocean_roughness.md), the sea-ice exponential relation and the vegetation-table blends. The neutral drag coefficient is the square of the von Kármán constant divided by the logarithmic height ratio. Each initial stress is density times its wind component times wind speed times that coefficient, with left-to-right multiplication.

For each tile, divide the heat flux by heat capacity. Buoyancy is gravity times the negative moisture and temperature contributions, divided by density. The squared friction velocity is the stress-vector magnitude divided by density. Positive buoyancy adds `0.002` times the power of buoyancy times boundary-layer height with exponent `RN(2/3)`. Take the hardware square root, apply the friction-velocity floor, then form dimensionless height from negative geopotential, the von Kármán constant and buoyancy divided by gravity times the cubed friction velocity. The cube is two ordered multiplications.

Ocean momentum roughness is viscosity divided by friction velocity plus Charnock coefficient divided by gravity times squared friction velocity. The sea-state heat and moisture branch uses the two roots of the declared quadratic. For wave roughness `A` and viscous roughness `H`, the source roots are `-(A+0.5*H) ± sqrt(A*A+0.25*H*H)`. Each absolute root is raised to its source weight, and the product has a floor of `1e-7 m`. Every root, weight and power call follows the original ordering. The mathematical weights are in zero to one, and the source rounding is retained without clipping. The mathematical root product supplies an independent cancellation-resistant physical check.

Sea-ice momentum roughness is the larger of its floor and `(1-c)*0.00093 + 0.00605*exp(-17*(c-0.5)*(c-0.5))`, with the square formed before multiplication by negative seventeen. Land roughness uses the complete original low, high, soil and snow table blends. Lake roughness selects the already computed open-water or ice result. Apply any whole-array fixed roughness assignment before resistance.

Resistance uses the logarithm of height divided by moisture roughness, then the full stable or unstable heat-profile correction at the top and at roughness height. Stable correction is `-B*(eta-C)*exp(-D*eta) - (1+A*eta)*sqrt(1+A*eta) - E + 1`. Unstable correction is `2*log((1+sqrt(1-F*eta))*0.5)`. Read the genuine unchanged stability constants from `YOS_EXCS`. Divide the corrected logarithm by friction velocity times the von Kármán constant. No trigonometric profile is consumed by this parent.
