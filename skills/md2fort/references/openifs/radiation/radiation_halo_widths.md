# Radiation Halo Widths

A halo is the surrounding grid region needed to interpolate a field near a process boundary. This SUECRAD component calculates north, south, east and west widths for interpolation into and out of the radiation grid, then publishes the local radiation halo rows. Widths and row bounds are integer grid counts.

The entry `md2asm_publish_runtime_radiation_halo_widths` accepts Geometry and its byte capacity in x0 and x1, MODEL and capacity in x2 and x3, an angular-extrema record and capacity in x4 and x5, and the completed public partition and capacity in x6 and x7. Geometry has 20,400 bytes, MODEL has 4,275,088 bytes, extrema has 64 bytes and the partition has 4,096 bytes. Records are mapped, nonwrapping and aligned to eight bytes. MODEL is disjoint from borrowed records and complete map owners. Extrema stores radiation minimum/maximum latitude and longitude, followed by the four main-grid extrema, in degrees. The caller retains their qualified coordinate producer and process association.

The completed partition marker is 3. Its north–south region count at byte 48 lies in 1 through 64, and its live region coordinate comes from byte 932. Main NDGLG at Geometry byte 4,872 is even in 2 through 65,536. Radiation NDGLG at RADGRID byte 52 is even in 2 through 8,192. Main NLOENG, NFRSTLAT and NLSTLAT descriptors are at Geometry bytes 7,640, 6,456 and 6,520. Radiation descriptors are at RADGRID bytes 152, 280 and 344. Region row vectors span 1 through the region count. Longitude-count vectors span -1 through N+2 with the original offset 1. Selected region endpoints lie in 1 through N, with positive endpoint row lengths at most 2N+16. The caller proves complete canonical descriptor headers, owner spans and disjointness. The entry checks consumed shape fields, selected rows and count bounds.

The original minimum halo is 5. For each direction, the source scales an absolute angular difference by N/180 for latitude, or by the larger endpoint row length divided by 360 for longitude. Each FP64 subtraction, division and multiplication rounds separately. The source computes both CEILING and FLOOR, then its comparison chooses 5+CEILING or max(0, 5-FLOOR). The reverse interpolation uses radiation dimensions and the original reversed comparison. Eight private width words are calculated before public publication. The final two row bounds use the source MAX/MIN expressions.

YRRI and YRRO start at MODEL bytes 4,122,568 and 4,124,368 and each has 1,800 bytes. Their north, south, east and west width words are at bytes 1,348, 1,352, 1,356 and 1,360. RADGRID starts at MODEL byte 4,098,608, with output local halo bounds at bytes 44 and 48. Inputs use NDGSAL 1, NDGSAG -1, NDGENG N+2, NDGSUR 2, a nonnegative offset below N, and a nonempty local ordinary-row interval. NRADINT is 2 or 3. Degree inputs are finite and ordered, with latitude in [-90,90] and longitude in [0,360]. FPCR is zero. Return 0 means success and 2 means an atomic refusal, with public bytes unchanged. There are no external calls or allocations. The 64-byte aligned private stack and all required saved registers, including x18, obey the Arm ABI.

One unchanged selected-source binary and one assembly object pass nine cases, including actual main/radiation counts 2,560/800, smaller 64/32, both comparison directions, equal zero, fractional values, both sides of an integer threshold, and minimum and maximum bounds. All 38,475,792 complete MODEL data bytes and 216 raw status bytes agree, with 324 independent 90-digit integer and row-bound relations per route. Nine nominal and 19 atomic-refusal native calls preserve their guards and borrowed inputs. Natural FPCR, FPSR and errno agree, including incoming flag values 0 and 24.

The verification partition is a three-scalar projection of an accepted public context, containing its completion marker, region count and region coordinate. No saved handle is consumed. Production must bind the actual live owned partition. Accepted extrema and map banks are reused without upstream replay. SLCSET, exchange, extrapolation, whole SUECRAD, RADDRV and the autonomous forecast remain separate.
