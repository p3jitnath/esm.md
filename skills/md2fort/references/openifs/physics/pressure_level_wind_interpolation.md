# Pressure Level Wind Interpolation Contract

The [physical note](../../../../../formulations/openifs/physics/pressure_level_wind_interpolation.md) supplies the wind profile and requested pressure levels. The original routines are PPUV and PPITPQ in arpifs/pp_obs/ppuv.F90 and arpifs/pp_obs/ppitpq.F90. The current shared source also retains the unchanged two-target PPFLEV, log-pressure PPINTP and CAPESHEAR bodies. PPUV and PPITPQ are the newly accepted scoped support bodies; the other entries gain no separate credit here.

The frozen required domain has a backing width of 24 columns, selected columns 1 through 24 or 1 through 8, 19 model levels, two output targets, first output level one, four pressure methods and PPITPQ selector KSL equal to four. Method four contains the full-level logarithms. This is a complete calculation for that frozen branch scope, rather than a claim about every pressure method or shape in the general source.

PPUV forms the source upper wind continuation, performs log-pressure interpolation, retains the constant lowest-level rule, and then calls PPITPQ for both wind components when the source control requires it. The recorded LRPPUV_CSTEXT and LRPPUV_CALLITPQ controls are zero and one. The old preflight that required every quadratic cell to be inactive is removed. The earlier identity-named child symbol is now a binding alias to the complete quadratic body; it does not select a numerical fallback.

For method four, PPITPQ uses the wind and logarithmic coordinates at full levels 1, 2 and 3. Write their coordinates as $`x_1`$, $`x_2`$ and $`x_3`$, their field values as $`f_1`$, $`f_2`$ and $`f_3`$, and the requested logarithmic coordinate as $`q`$. Only $`q`$ strictly below $`x_2`$ replaces the incoming interpolated value. The original ordered formula is:

```math
D_1=(x_2-x_1)(x_1-x_3),\qquad D_2=(x_2-x_3)(x_1-x_3).
f(q)=f_2+\frac{(f_2-f_1)(q-x_2)(q-x_3)}{D_1}-\frac{(f_2-f_3)(q-x_1)(q-x_2)}{D_2}.
```

Compute each denominator with its displayed subtraction order. Compute each correction from left to right, divide separately, add the first correction to $`f_2`$, then subtract the second. Every operation rounds separately in FP64. Distinct finite coordinate nodes are required for the active physical branch. Equality and greater targets preserve the incoming output bits. The strict source comparison also sets the invalid-operation status flag for an unordered quiet-NaN comparison and skips its output write; that diagnostic status case is verified separately from the finite physical domain.

The Arm application binary interface passes 17 ordinary addresses for PPUV and 12 for PPITPQ, with no hidden lengths. The first eight use x0 through x7 and the others use incoming stack slots. Their frames use 12288 and 4096 bytes with 16-byte alignment. They retain the required saved integer and floating-point registers. These explicit-shape arrays are raw column-first owners: wind and output values use eight-byte FP64 words, while index and logical words have four bytes. Pressure and scratch field storage include the original level-zero plane; pressure-method owners cover 24 by 20 by 4 values. All complete owners and original lower bounds must remain available, including inactive columns and output cells.

The source keeps the qualified pressure-bracket and linear-interpolation children, original hooks and live module controls. Accepted numerical child proofs remain required. The shared source has no includes and changes only publication comments from the current ready body. FPCR must be zero, with nearest-even rounding, gradual underflow and signed zeros. Fused operations, reassociation, contraction and approximate arithmetic remain forbidden.

The new critical receipt passes eight PPITPQ cases with constant, linear, quadratic and quiet-NaN profiles in full and partial rows, plus two genuine-backed active PPUV cases. All 240328 complete compared bytes match, raw status and native ABI match, and 126 active values pass an independent 80-digit three-point Lagrange check. It also retains 130 inactive or unordered cells and the equality boundary bytes. The previous full/partial PPUV proof had zero active quadratic cells, so that receipt alone could not accept this branch.

The independent peer also passes the original operation order and 12-address child ABI. It recomputes all 126 active cells with 80-digit Lagrange weights and verifies 258 inactive, unordered or nonselected raw words. Its largest absolute error is 4.805e-14 metres per second, at most 6.45 percent of its tighter rounding bound $`\gamma_{16}(\lvert f_2\rvert+\lvert T_1\rvert+\lvert T_2\rvert)`$ for the tested normal finite inputs. Here $`T_1`$ and $`T_2`$ are the two correction terms, $`u=2^{-53}`$ is the FP64 unit roundoff, and $`\gamma_{16}=16u/(1-16u)`$. The source, status and ABI acceptance limit remains exact. The retained complete-parent, raw-owner, immediate-status and ABI proofs combine with the passing joint final-state and ten-file gate to admit this frozen branch scope. The original Fortran stays a verification reference; declared source metadata services remain temporary verification dependencies. The exact inventory places both accepted bodies in main and local physical-output groups, outside the weather list. Autonomous forecast closure remains unproved.
